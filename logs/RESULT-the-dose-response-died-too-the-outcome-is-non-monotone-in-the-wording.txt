📌 THE PRACTICAL CONCLUSION, WHICH IS STRONGER THAN THE MECHANISM I FAILED TO FIND
    A spec rule inferred from a single A/B on this line is UNRELIABLE, because neighbouring
    wordings of the same content land on different outcomes: "Sentinel error values, all matchable
    via errors.Is" gives a truncated name, and "Sentinel error values, all readily matchable with
    errors.Is" gives no name at all. That is exactly how 6 August's rule came to be published —
    one A/B, no placebo, no neighbouring wordings — and it is why it described the wrong
    ingredient. The generalisable lesson is not about sentinels: BEFORE SHIPPING ANY PROMPT OR
    SPEC RULE, DRAW ITS NEIGHBOURS. If two paraphrases of the intended rule disagree, what was
    measured is the wording, not the rule.
