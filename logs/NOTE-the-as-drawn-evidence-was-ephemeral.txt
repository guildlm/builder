AND IT PUTS A PRICE ON THE QUEUED src/ CHANGE
NOTE-the-second-separating-draw-is-not-ready.txt proposes dumping `written` to
`<out>/.pre-fix.json` at the head of _fix_loop. Until now the argument for it was that it
UNBLOCKS an experiment. The stronger argument is that it converts a whole class of evidence
this repo already relies on from ephemeral to durable, at three lines, and would have made all
three checks above permanent records rather than recollections.

    It also removes the race. Snapshotting by hand means copying a tree while the fix loop is
    writing to it. That worked; it is not a method anyone should have to repeat.

⚠️ METHOD NOTE, and it nearly produced a wrong answer in this very check
The first pass used `ls -d generated/*asdrawn* generated/*as-drawn* generated/*snapshot*`.
In zsh, a failed glob ABORTS THE WHOLE COMMAND — the first pattern matched nothing, so the
other two were never evaluated, and the shell printed one error while my `|| echo "no snapshot
trees"` fallback printed a confident conclusion. The conclusion happened to be right. The
method could not have established it.

    A multi-pattern `ls` in zsh reports on the first pattern and silently abandons the rest.
    Use `find` with -o, which evaluates every branch and cannot half-run.

The same shape appeared twice more today: a substring check that "found" a preserved tree in
`expreval-v4` ("ex-PREV-al"), and `pgrep -f "guildlm-build main"` matching the shells that
merely mention it. Three variants of one failure in one day — the query was right and the
world was not what the query assumed.
