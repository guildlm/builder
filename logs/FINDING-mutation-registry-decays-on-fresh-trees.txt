FINDING — the mutation registry is pinned to the ARCHIVE and decays silently
============================================================================
2026-07-25. Found in my own result, one hour after documenting the same shape elsewhere.

HOW IT SURFACED
  Grading the usersapi closure, the "must not regress" section printed

        store.go   duplicate User ID -> ErrExists (409)      NOAPPLY
        store.go   List returns users sorted by ID           NOAPPLY

  Under a heading that says must not regress, two NOAPPLY rows read as nothing regressed.
  They mean the check DID NOT RUN.

MEASURED, TEXT ONLY, NO GO AND NO MODEL
  Against the ARCHIVE the registry is healthy:
        30 registered · 29 apply · 1 dead by design (ledger keeps a store.go/memory.go
        either-or pair for the same invariant, only one of which exists in a given run)
        · 1 special-cased

  Against FRESHLY GENERATED trees it decays, and unevenly:
        usersapi    2 live on the archive  ->  0 live on the fresh tree
        ratelimit   1 live on the archive  ->  1 live on the fresh tree

  So ratelimit's CAUGHT was a real non-regression result and usersapi's was nothing at
  all — and the two printed in the same format, which is why I published the usersapi
  closure with the caveat but only found the magnitude afterwards.

WHY IT DECAYS
  Every mutation is an EXACT string lifted from the artifact it was registered against.
  A fresh coder writes the same behaviour differently — a renamed receiver, a reordered
  guard, one line instead of three — and the patch stops applying. Nothing is wrong with
  the code and nothing is wrong with the registry; they have simply drifted apart, and
  drift is invisible because its output is the absence of findings.

FIXED IN BOTH TOOLS, EACH PROVEN BOTH WAYS
  _hole_closed  now reports "N ran, M did not apply" and EXITS NONZERO when none ran,
                with the message that this is not "no regression", it is no check.
                Fires: usersapi's fresh tree, rc=1. Does not over-fire: ratelimit's fresh
                tree still rc=0.
  _teeth_suite  the summary line now states how many mutations ACTUALLY RAN, and a
                selection where none ran exits nonzero. Its headline was "0 invariant(s)
                UNDEFENDED", which is also what a suite that ran nothing prints.
                Fires: reformatting the guarded block onto ONE LINE — still compiling, so
                the baseline stays green and NOAPPLY is isolated from BASELINE-RED, which
                exits 1 too and would have let the proof pass for the wrong reason —
                gives "0/1 actually ran", rc=1. Restored: "1/1", rc=0.

THE RESIDUE
  A verdict registry is a measurement instrument with a shelf life, and its expiry is
  silent by construction. Any check that reports per-item verdicts needs a denominator —
  how many items were examined — beside the numerator, or "0 problems" and "0 examined"
  compress to the same sentence. That is the third form of this failure found today, after
  four instruments answering about nothing and an audit comparing a live spec to a frozen
  artifact.
