1. taskapipro — DOES AN ATTACHED REASON BUY AN EXACT QUANTITY?
--------------------------------------------------------------
The edit: the projects paging entry now carries its OWN measured reason rather than a
cross-reference to the tasks entry three sentences above it.

What it is repairing: on chain4 the projects paging test POSTed ONE project and asserted
`len == 1`. Page size 2 and page size 7777 both return one item from a store of one, so no
input can distinguish them — the three paging sites in projects.go SURVIVED because the
witness could not fail, not because the test was missing. The twin tasks test, whose entry
carried ten lines of reason, was CAUGHT.

PREDICT: the projects paging test POSTs THREE projects and asserts EXACTLY 2.       ~70%
PREDICT: the three paging sites in projects.go flip SURVIVED -> CAUGHT.             ~60%

The second is lower than the first ON PURPOSE and the gap is the interesting part. POSTing
three is necessary for the sites to flip; it is not sufficient. A test can post three, ask
for page size 2, and still assert something that a clamped limit also satisfies. If the
first lands and the second does not, the finding is that prose bought the SETUP and not the
ASSERTION, which is a sharper result than either clean outcome.

Leaning yes because of the shortener finding from the same week: a sentence that forbids an
assertion while also stating the correct value gets read FOR THE VALUE. The reason states
the quantity. The same reading behaviour that defeated the prohibition should serve here.

REFUTED BY: the test POSTs one project again, or posts three and asserts a number a clamp
also produces. Either kills "attached reason is a per-test cost payable at every test" and
promotes the alternative on the table: an exact quantity needs a mechanism that is not
prose — a fixture, a table, a named constant the test must reference.
