THE HYPOTHESIS BEING TESTED (from the 17:15 FINDING)
The ledger spec lists internal/store/store.go SIXTH and internal/store/memory.go SEVENTH. Files
generate in spec order and each prompt carries already-generated siblings' CONTENT. So store.go goes
first; when it writes the MemStore implementation, memory.go is generated next, finds the
implementation already in its sibling block, and ships fourteen bytes.

    The harness already forbids this in words — scope_rule (builder.py:995, injected at 1083) names
    "the in-memory implementation" as its example and says doing another file's job "leaves that file
    nothing to declare, it ships EMPTY". It fails in 58% of draws corpus-wide, 67% for ledger.
