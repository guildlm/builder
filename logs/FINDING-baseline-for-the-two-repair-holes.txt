FINDING — baseline for the two repair holes: 64 of 125 trees ship an empty planned file
=======================================================================================
03:30 on 31 July. Zero GPU. The baseline the 03:10 finding said had to exist before either fix is
applied — because today's standing rule is that instruments do not change until the change can be
scored against something.

THE CORPUS, static check over every generated tree of the three affected specs
    125 trees examined
      64  HAVE an empty planned .go file  (51%)
      61  do not

    Of the 64 empty-file instances, what would `_fill_empty_planned_files` do TODAY:
      54  (84%)  `take` is NON-EMPTY — the existing repair identifies symbols to move, and a donor
                 sibling that actually declares them. HOLE 2 does not block these.
      10  (16%)  `take` is EMPTY — the pass no-ops silently. EVERY ONE is ledger's
                 internal/store/memory.go with required={'Store'}, which store.go legitimately owns.

WHAT THAT APPORTIONS
    HOLE 2 (the regex) accounts for 10 of 64, and they are all one shape: ledger writes the type as
    `MemStore` in backticks, never "MemStore struct", so _REQUIRED_TYPE_RE cannot see it and the only
    name it extracts is Store — which the donor is supposed to keep. Narrow, specific, and fixable
    by broadening the extractor to backticked capitalised identifiers.

    HOLE 1 (green-gating) is where the other 54 live. For those the repair KNOWS what to move and
    has a donor holding it — so the reason they shipped empty is that the pass either never ran
    (project not green, `_finish_green` never called) or ran and was reverted because the whole
    project failed `toolchain.check` afterwards for unrelated reasons.

    ⚠️ I CANNOT SPLIT THOSE TWO SUB-CASES FROM THE CORPUS. Old trees carry no logs, and the only
    evidence distinguishing "never ran" from "ran and reverted" is the pass's own log lines. Today's
    five A draws do split: ledger p6/p7/p8 were green and logged the WARNING (pass ran, no-op'd via
    hole 2); workapi and taskapipro were not green and logged nothing at all (pass never ran). That
    is n=5 and it is consistent with both holes being live, which is what the 03:10 reading said.

WHAT THE BASELINE IS FOR
    Fixing HOLE 1 — run the empty-file check regardless of green — has an addressable population of
    up to 54 of 64 instances. That is the bigger lever by a wide margin.
    Fixing HOLE 2 — broaden the extractor — addresses 10, and is the only fix that helps ledger,
    which is the spec this campaign draws most.

    ⚠️ "ADDRESSABLE" IS NOT "WOULD BE FIXED". For the 54, the move is identified but the green-check
    still has to pass afterwards, and on an already-broken tree it may not. The honest prediction is
    that fixing hole 1 converts some unknown fraction of 54 from "silent" to "warned", and a smaller
    fraction from "empty" to "repaired". The warning alone is worth it: today two of five A draws
    shipped a dead file with NOTHING in the log saying so.
