PREREG — does the order fix TRANSFER to a second spec? workapi, chosen for its extreme baseline
===============================================================================================
Written 21:50 on 30 July, before the draw.

WHY workapi AND NOT ledger AGAIN
Three ledger pairs all came back A-stub / B-full. More ledger pairs raise the ratio; they do not
test whether the finding is about LEDGER or about the PIPELINE. workapi does, and it is the right
second spec for a reason that is about power, not variety:

    stub rate, deduplicated by (memory.go, store.go) content pair:
        workapi     12 of 15   80%     <- most extreme
        ledger       4 of  6   67%
        taskapipro   6 of 17   35%

    The 18:30 asymmetry lesson: test where the baseline is EXTREME, not where it is 50/50. At an 80%
    stub rate a single non-stubbing B is roughly 5:1 on its own, where the same observation on
    taskapipro would be worth almost nothing.

THE MANIPULATION — identical in kind to the ledger pairs
    specs/workapi-order1.yaml: the store.go and memory.go entry BLOCKS transposed, nothing else.
    29451 chars == 29451 · 29561 bytes == 29561 · whole-file character multiset UNCHANGED
    workapi lists them in the SAME order every affected spec does — store.go 6th, memory.go 7th —
    which is why the corpus could never test this (17:45 finding).

SAME GATED DESIGN, and the gate matters more here
    draw A = specs/workapi.yaml (original order). If A does NOT stub, DISCARD and do not draw B.
    draw B = specs/workapi-order1.yaml, same process, only if A stubbed.

    At 80% the gate should reject about one process in five, versus one in three for ledger.

PREDICTION — given A stubs, B is FULL: 85%
    Higher than the 75% used for the first ledger pair, and the reason is evidence rather than
    enthusiasm: the prohibition "B cannot stub" has now survived three independent tests on three
    processes. That is what should move a number.

    ⚠️ Mechanism predictions in this record are 0 for 7 — but this is no longer a mechanism STORY,
    it is a mechanism with three paired replications. The 0-for-7 counts arguments I could not test;
    this one has been tested. Stating that distinction because otherwise the caveat becomes a
    ritual that ignores its own evidence.

BRANCHES
    A STUBS · B FULL   the fix TRANSFERS. The finding is about the pipeline, not about ledger, and
                       reordering becomes a general recommendation for every spec with an
                       interface/implementation pair.
    A STUBS · B STUBS  the fix is LEDGER-SPECIFIC, and the prohibition is broken on its fourth test.
                       This would be the single most informative outcome available tonight.
    A FULL             uninformative pair, discard, retry on a new process (~1 in 5 expected).
    UNMEASURED         no snapshot. Redraw.

⚠️ NOT PROMOTING THE FIX TO specs/ledger.yaml YET, and the reason is the experiment itself: the
paired design needs the ORIGINAL order as its A. Editing ledger.yaml would remove the control that
makes future ledger pairs possible. The fix is recorded and ready; promotion waits until the
evidence is judged sufficient, which is a separate decision from measuring it.
