ADDENDUM, same session — WHAT THE 9 FATAL TREES ACTUALLY COST, and it is not an inference
The 9 were identified by a dangling reference on disk. A dangling reference guarantees a compile
failure, but "present in a failed build" is not "the reason it failed", so the logs were opened.
Eight of the nine have a findable log (ledger-ctl-proc3 does not):

    ...and it is the LAST undefined error in the log          8 of 8

So it is not merely present: it is the error each of these builds was still failing on when it
ran out of rounds. One identifier is the terminal cause of eight failed builds in this corpus.

⚠️ 'TERMINAL CAUSE' IS NOT 'ONLY CAUSE'. These builds hit other errors earlier and repaired them;
what this shows is that the sentinel is what remained. Removing it does not guarantee eight
greens — it removes the thing that was still standing at the end.
