WHAT COLD IS NOT

It is tempting — and it is what I assumed for the first ten minutes — to read COLD as "not a
hole". That reading is exactly backwards for the sharpest holes in this corpus.

    taskflow  tasks_handler.go:72   writeJSON(w, http.StatusNotFound, nil)   COLD
    workapi   internal/api/tasks.go:93  writeJSON(w, http.StatusNotFound, nil)   COLD

Both are Delete's own 404. Both specs promise it in the handler's own comment. Both suites
contain a test named for the delete that asserts a 404 — from the GET that follows a
SUCCESSFUL delete. taskflow's is the mirror I closed this week; workapi's was found today by
this tool and closed an hour ago. In both cases COLD is not the reason it is not a hole. COLD
IS THE HOLE: not one weak assertion, but no request at all.

So:

    COLD + reachable in production   = the strongest kind of hole. Nothing even goes there.
    COLD + unreachable               = dead code. Not a hole, and never was.

The 37 sentinel-else 500s are the second: the capstone established that these artifacts wire
a concrete in-memory store which returns only the sentinels the `if` already handles, so no
input reaches the `else`. The 4 cold 404s are the first — DELETE a missing id and you are
there immediately.

    COLD is an AMPLIFIER on a candidate, not a dismissal of one. What discriminates is
    production reachability, and coverage cannot see that: it measures the SUITE's reach,
    not the program's.

WHY THIS MATTERS NOW

Three times this corpus settled a row by asking "does this line run" — the 17 dead 500s,
expreval's consumeOperator bound, tasks-api's subsumed Title guard — and each time it cost a
coverage run or a code-read PER ROW. That was affordable at 48 candidates. It is not
affordable at what the sweep is about to become: five of six shapes were skipping internal/
entirely, and workapi alone goes from 6 rows to 36 once the walk is fixed.

One test run per artifact answers the reachability half for every site in it at once, BEFORE
any mutation is applied. The sweep still has to run — a cold site still needs its SURVIVED
row for the accounting — but the adjudication that follows is now a join, not 60 code-reads.

THE HONEST LIMIT

Coverage is measured on the suite AS IT STANDS. Every closure this week ADDS a test, so a
site that is cold today may be warm in the next draw — which is the point of the closures,
and it means this table is a snapshot of the -v4 corpus and not a property of the specs.
Re-run it after the redraw; the cold count going DOWN is a result about the campaign, and
the one number here I would actually like to watch.


CROSS-VALIDATED AGAINST THE MUTATION SWEEP (2026-07-29, during the v4 re-sweep)

The premise of this tool is that a mutation on a never-executed line cannot be caught. That
was an argument; here is the measurement. taskapipro's internal/config/config.go, %w shape:

    reachability   L36 run      L44 COLD   L52 COLD   L60 COLD   L68 COLD
    sweep          5 rows, ALL SURVIVED

    COLD => SURVIVED   holds, 4 of 4.
    SURVIVED => COLD   does NOT. L36 is executed and survives anyway.

That asymmetry is the whole value and it is now data rather than reasoning. It splits the
survivor list into two populations that need completely different work:

    COLD + SURVIVED   pre-answered. No assertion could have caught it. Adjudicate by asking
                      whether the shipped composition can REACH the line at all — dead
                      branch (the 500-else class) or a real hole nothing requests (Delete's
                      404). One coverage run per artifact answers the first half for every
                      site at once.

    WARM + SURVIVED   the sharp set. A test EXECUTES this line and asserts nothing about
                      what it produced. This is the project's founding observation —
                      coverage measures lines RUN, not invariants DEFENDED — and it is now
                      mechanically locatable instead of being found one row at a time.

PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE for the post-sweep triage: compute WARM+SURVIVED first. It is the
smaller set and every member of it is a candidate that no cheap check can dismiss.

A LIMIT ON THE JOIN, stated because it bounds the above: the sweep's rows carry
(artifact, file, shape, verdict) and no LINE NUMBER, while reachability's carry lines. So a
file whose sites are mixed cold and warm cannot be joined exactly — only bounded (if a
file+shape has k cold sites and m SURVIVED rows, at least m-k warm sites survived). Adding
the line to the sweep's rows would make the join exact and is a small change. NOT made now:
the sweep is running, and changing the tool mid-run would leave rows that disagree with the
tool that wrote them.
