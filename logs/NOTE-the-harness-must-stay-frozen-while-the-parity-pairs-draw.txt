THE NEAR MISS
The stripper finding was weakened an hour ago, and what would settle the open version is a one-line
change: log the NAMES `strip_redeclarations` removes. Cheap, low risk, and it makes the next
occurrence self-documenting. I was about to make it.

    `_parity_ab.sh` invokes `.venv/bin/guildlm-build` ONCE PER DRAW — a fresh Python process every
    time, which re-reads builder.py. A change made now would land in draws 2 through 6 and NOT in
    draw 1, which already ran.

    The entire design of this A/B is that control and arm share a commit. logs/PREREG-withhold-...
    says so in those words, and the switch exists instead of a revert for exactly this reason. A
    logging change is harmless to behaviour and fatal to the guarantee.

    obvious guard (record git HEAD at start, refuse a draw if it moved) until the run finishes.
