THE TWO ACCOUNTS THIS DRAW SEPARATES (stated first so the result cannot be fitted afterwards)

    (A) WORDING-DETERMINISTIC. A given wording has a fixed outcome; the map is a property of the
        wording, and pid 4970's table is the table. Predicts F1 LONG and F2 ABSENT again on p5.

    (B) RATE. Size sets a PROBABILITY that an arbitrary rewrite lands. Within a process a wording
        is frozen (which is why redraws reproduce), but across processes it is a FRESH DRAW.
        Predicts F1 and F2 are each ~a coin weighted by the +15..+19 rate, independent of what
        happened on p4 — most likely BOTH ABSENT.

    ⚠️ THE EXTREMES CANNOT SEPARATE THESE AND I AM NOT COUNTING THEM AS EVIDENCE. paraphrase +20
    is LONG on 3 of 3 and L6 +14 is ABSENT on 4 of 4, but (B) predicts exactly that too, because
    its rate is ~1 at the top and ~0.13 at the bottom. The ONLY discriminating evidence in the
    whole archive is the MIDDLE of the scale, and there the single data point favours (B):
    L4 (+5) is ABBREVIATED on p1, LONG on p2, ABBREVIATED on p3.

    Weight going in: (B) 0.65 / (A) 0.35. I am drawing the arms I expect to fail to replicate.

PROTOCOL (both gates unchanged, in this order, no exceptions)
    1. Probe fresh processes with specs/ledger-origorder-baseline.yaml until one returns ABSENT.
       Max 10 probes. LONG is uninformative. ABBREVIATED-at-baseline is informative under the
       standing definition but DISCARDED here: this pair is a movement FROM ABSENT and cannot be
       read from halfway up. Every probe, kept AND discarded, goes in logs/PROBE-LEDGER.txt.
    2. SCREEN with specs/ledger-ownplacebo.yaml (+54 rendered, content-free, SAME purpose, larger
       than every arm below). A process that flips is DISCARDED and recorded. Only
       ABSENT-at-baseline AND screen-null may host arms.
    3. On the eligible process, RESTART=0 throughout, IN THIS ORDER:

           base (= the probe from step 1) · screen +54
           · L6 +14 (low anchor) · paraphrase +20 (high anchor)
           · F1 +15 · F2 +17
           · base-close
           · [budget] F4 +20 · F3 +19 · base-close-2

       ANCHORS FIRST, unchanged from 13 August: without them on THIS process the rungs are
       uninterpretable, and a session that runs out of budget must end with the anchors drawn.
       F1 leads the pair because it is the arm both sharp contrasts run through.
       base-close sits BEFORE the optional arms so that the registered four are guarded even if
       the session ends there.

BRANCHES — written now so no result can be read into whatever I hoped for
    F1 LONG and F2 ABSENT  (the contrast reproduces)
        -> account (A) gains sharply and MY REGISTERED WEIGHT WAS WRONG. A one-word difference in
           a fixed slot would then be a PORTABLE property of the wording, which makes the p4 pair
           the most mechanically informative object in the campaign and the next draws belong on
           the words themselves, not on more processes. I would report this as the result that
           most damages the account I am working from.
    both ABSENT  (the contrast vanishes downward)
        -> account (B). F1's LONG was a property of pid 4970, not of "all reliably". The 13 August
           headline stands and STRENGTHENS: the sharpest pair in the campaign does not survive a
           change of process, so a spec rule read off ONE process is unreliable even when it was
           drawn twice byte-identical. The practical rule hardens: determinism within a process is
           NOT evidence of portability, and every published one-process contrast is downgraded.
    both LONG  (the contrast vanishes upward)
        -> F2's ABSENT was the process-specific half. Same conclusion as above for (B), reached
           from the other side, and it additionally says the gap's rate is much higher on p5 than
           on p4 — which would make PROCESS, not size and not wording, the dominant term. That
           deserves its own prereg and I will not extend the session to chase it.
    F2 above F1  (reversal)
        -> the strongest arbitrariness result available here: the ordering is not even stable in
           sign across processes. Redraw BOTH before writing anything down.
    anchors do NOT reproduce (L6 not ABSENT, or paraphrase not LONG)
        -> report the pair against THIS process's anchors only, flag the series as
           anchor-conditional, and do NOT relabel any arm against p4's anchors.
    F1 or F2 lands ABBREVIATED
        -> the gap gets its first three-valued row; report it as a rung of the scale and NOT as a
           weak LONG. "Above ABSENT" is the tally that moves, "LONG" is not.

REDRAW RULE, REGISTERED IN ADVANCE RATHER THAN AFTER THE SURPRISE
    Any arm whose verdict DIFFERS from its pid-4970 verdict is redrawn once, RESTART=0, before it
    is written into any log. This is not optional and it is not conditional on which direction the
    difference goes — 13 August had to amend its own prereg mid-session to add exactly this, and
    registering it up front is the cheaper version of that lesson.

VOID
    · repair activity in any arm's log (the probe already refuses on it)
    · a server restart between arms
    · base-close differing from base
    · no eligible process within 10 probes -> that IS the result, recorded, and no arm is run

⚠️ NOT A PREDICTION, A CONSTRAINT: probes are ~3.5-5 minutes and finding an eligible process has
cost 1 to 12 draws across the last four sessions. If the budget runs out, the ANCHORS and the PAIR
are what will have run; F3 and F4 are explicitly optional and their absence is not a gap.

⚠️ SCORING IS AS-DRAWN and the table is assembled by `_arm_table.py --prefix p5- --pid <PID>`,
which cross-checks four independent records per arm and REFUSES on any mismatch. The pooled counts
come from `_size_floor_tally.py`. Nothing in the result log is hand-counted — that rule exists
because hand counting has produced a wrong number eight times in this campaign.
