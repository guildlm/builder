THE QUESTION THE PAIRS SETTLE
    Two accounts survive the singles and they differ in what a pair does:

    THRESHOLD  what matters is HOW MANY concrete `ErrExists` mentions the store purposes carry.
               Baseline 3, singles 2, pairs 1, bundle 0. If the threshold sits at two removals,
               some pair flips.
    LAST-ONE   what matters is whether ANY concrete store-side mention SURVIVES. One is as good
               as three at holding the declarer in its pruning behaviour, and only the complete
               absence changes anything. Then NO pair flips.

    P(no pair flips — the last mention has to go) = 0.50
    P(ab flips) = 0.30    P(ac flips) = 0.25    P(bc flips) = 0.25
    P(all three pairs flip) = 0.15

    I lean to LAST-ONE precisely because the singles were not merely small — they were ZERO. A
    dose that does nothing at one third and nothing at two thirds is more likely a switch than a
    slope.
