WHAT THIS DOES TO THE HEADLINE
"Redraw drift" was measured across a corpus in which most of the flip-bearing specs had been
substantially rewritten. With the spec held constant, the observed drift across 33 live rows
is ZERO — which is exactly what every same-condition draw comparison today has shown
(taskapipro 0 of 8 and 0 of 7, taskflow 0 of 12).

    SO 6-UP-AND-2-DOWN IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF REDRAW DRIFT. It is a measurement of
    SPEC-EDIT drift, and the campaign's own baseline for "what a redraw moves on its own"
    should be closer to zero.

⚠️ AND IT IS CONFOUNDED IN A WAY THAT CANNOT BE UNDONE FROM THIS DATA. The specs grew BECAUSE
closures were added to them. So "grew >=40 lines" is the same set as "the campaign worked on
it". A flip in taskflow may be:
        (a) the closure taking effect — the intended result; or
        (b) 73 added lines perturbing an unrelated file — an artefact;
and nothing measured here separates them. That is the finding: THE CAMPAIGN CANNOT
DISTINGUISH ITS OWN SUCCESSES FROM ITS OWN PERTURBATION, and until today it had no reason to
think there was a perturbation to worry about.

⚠️ THE ZERO IS SUGGESTIVE, NOT SIGNIFICANT. If the 7.5% rate applied to the unchanged-spec
rows, 33 rows would yield 2.5 expected flips and P(observing 0) = 0.077. That does not exclude
the null at any conventional threshold. What carries the argument is not the p-value but the
DIRECT measurement: +12 lines changes code in four of twelve files, with zero variance under
it, on two independent specs.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, and neither is run
  1. Redraw an artifact whose spec did NOT change (jsoncodec, kvservice, priorityqueue,
     ratelimit — all 0 lines) and sweep it. Predicted 0 flips. That measures redraw drift
     with the spec genuinely held constant, which the capstone never did.
  2. Add twelve INERT lines to one of those specs and sweep. If flips appear, spec growth
     alone moves mutation verdicts — which would make the artefact reading the leading one.

    (2) is the sharper test and it is one draw plus one targeted sweep.
