2. A THIRD NOISE SOURCE, and it is the program's OWN OUTPUT
    "moving MemStore into it breaks 2026/07/31 13:37:35 GET /accounts -> 200 (1.708µs)" is not an
    error. It is the ledger's request-logging middleware printing during `go test`, and it carries a
    WALL-CLOCK TIMESTAMP and a NANOSECOND DURATION — both different on every run.

    `_error_entries` keeps any line it cannot parse as file:line, so each of those lines is a
    "message". The after-set contains timestamps the before-set cannot contain, the count rises, and
    the move is refused for damage it did not do.

    That is the same class as the two already fixed today — goroutine IDs, then hex pointer
    arguments — and the third instance of it. `_canonical_toolchain_output` already strips per-test
    durations and `(cached)` for exactly this reason; it does not strip the program's own stdout.

    ⚠️ I WROTE "SO THE CORPUS NUMBERS ARE A FLOOR AGAIN" AND THEN MEASURED IT. They are not.

        builds segmented across the archive:                              654

        And v3 refused NOTHING — zero refusals across 74 cases — so no case was lost to this or to
        anything else. Noise of this kind causes false REFUSALS, never false accepts, so a run with
        zero refusals cannot have been reduced by it. 57 of 57 addressable stands as measured, not
        as a floor.

    WHY IT IS RARE: the lines only appear when the tests actually RUN. Most failing builds die at
    compile, so nothing executes and nothing prints. This one got far enough to serve requests. So
    the defect is real, live, and narrow — it cost exactly one repair, on one artifact, today.
