FINDING — the v5 sweep does not draw ledger, and ledger is 10% of the baseline
==============================================================================

Found while profiling the v4 baseline to precompute the comparable denominator, which
_sweep_v5.sh's own NEXT block calls the headline. Found BEFORE the diff was read, which is
the only reason it is a finding rather than a correction.

THE NUMBERS
    logs/hole-hunt-rows.tsv    314 rows across 17 artifacts
    _sweep_v5.sh SPECS         22 specs
    overlap                    16 artifacts

    in the v4 baseline, NOT drawn in v5:  ledger            (31 rows)
    drawn in v5, absent from v4 baseline: demo-small, eventbus, genericset,
                                          lrucache, walkv, workerpool

ledger is the FIFTH LARGEST artifact in the tracked baseline — 31 rows, ahead of tasks-api,
usersapi and shortener — and it is not in the sweep list. specs/ledger.yaml exists, is
24,813 bytes, the third largest spec in the repo, and was last touched 17 July.

WHY THIS IS NOT A ROUNDING ERROR
ledger is the artifact this entire programme is named after. TEETH.md opens on it: "a
ledger that drops every credit". _mutant_check.sh carries it as a canonical mutation
("every credit silently dropped (double-entry ledger loses money)"). It has its own
PREDICTION file. It is the founding example of a suite that goes green while the invariant
is undefended — and the capstone experiment meant to measure whether the corpus improved
does not draw it.

WHAT WOULD HAVE HAPPENED IF THIS WERE FOUND AFTER THE DIFF
_redraw_diff reports "N sites only in the old sweep" as a single number, described in its
own docstring as "a site that moved, appeared or vanished — which is a finding of its own
and not a verdict change". Thirty-one of those would have been ledger, and nothing in the
output distinguishes "this site moved to another file" from "this artifact was never
drawn". The comparable denominator would have read ~10% low, the honest-looking sentence
"31 sites present only in the old sweep" would have been printed, and the reason would have
been invisible. That sentence would have been TRUE and its SUBJECT would not have been what
it appeared to be — the sixth instance of the shape this session has been about, and the
first one caught before it did any damage rather than after.

THE OTHER TWO NON-DRAWN SPECS ARE CORRECTLY EXCLUDED
tasks-api-min and tasks-api-noshadownudge are ablation variants of tasks-api, built to vary
one spec feature at a time. Neither has any row in the v4 baseline, so neither can affect a
v4-vs-v5 comparison. Only ledger is an omission.

ACTION TAKEN
ledger is drawn as -v5 after workapi, by _sweep_v5_ledger.sh, before _hole_hunt.py --gen=v5
runs. This RECOVERS 31 comparable rows rather than documenting their loss. It costs one
draw against a GPU that is already running unattended.

⚠️ CAVEAT IT INHERITS: like taskapipro and workapi, ledger-v5 is drawn against the second
server process (see NOTE-v5-drawn-across-two-server-processes.txt). Three of the corpus's
23 artifacts now carry that condition instead of two.

⚠️ WHAT THIS DOES NOT FIX: the six artifacts drawn in v5 with no v4 counterpart
(demo-small, eventbus, genericset, lrucache, walkv, workerpool) stay uncomparable, and
correctly so — they are new to the corpus, not moved. They will show up as "only in the new
sweep" and that is the accurate description. Do not read their row count as growth in
holes; read it as growth in the corpus.
