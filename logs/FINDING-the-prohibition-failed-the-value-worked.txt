WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE GREEN TREE

If I had checked only the outcome — RED with fix rounds, then GREEN with none — I would have
concluded that withdrawing an option works, and filed it as confirmation of tonight's
healthz lesson ("explaining a trap is not a constraint; withdrawing the option is"). The
artifact says otherwise. The option was withdrawn and exercised anyway.

What actually worked was supplying the correct VALUE. That is the same shape as tonight's
other measured result, from the opposite direction: for a QUANTITY that must be exact, the
reason is what makes it stick, and the instruction alone does not. Here a PROHIBITION alone
did not stick either — and the information embedded in its justification did all the work.

    A spec sentence that forbids something and also states the right answer will be read for
    the answer. The prohibition is the part that gets dropped.
