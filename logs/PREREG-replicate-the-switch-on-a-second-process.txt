PREREG — does the switch cross the process boundary?
====================================================
10 August, committed BEFORE pid 60986 is killed and before any arm on the new process.
Follows 66fc9ab, whose entire result is n = ONE process.

WHY THIS IS THE NEXT JOB AND NOT THE OTHER-NAME ARM
    Twelve draws made the arms clean against each other, because draws are deterministic within
    a process. They say nothing about how many processes behave this way, and 31 July established
    that the declaration under test varies BY PROCESS. A within-process switch that does not cross
    the boundary is a curiosity about pid 60986; one that crosses is a finding about the model.
    Everything else queued is downstream of that answer.

PROTOCOL
    · Probe fresh processes with specs/ledger-origorder-baseline.yaml until one is INFORMATIVE
      (verdict ABSENT or ABBREVIATED). STOPPING RULE: at most 6 probes; if none is informative the
      replication does not happen today and that is recorded, not worked around.
    · Every probe, kept and discarded, goes in PROBE-LEDGER.txt. The claim stays conditional:
      "among processes that drop the name, ...". No rate may be computed from probes.
    · On the informative process, with RESTART=0 throughout:
          base   baseline
          bc     the pair that is the bundle's own length (+81) and was null on process A
          strip  the full bundle
          base-close  baseline again, last
    · `bc` is included rather than a single because it is the strongest null from process A: same
      length as the treatment, one mention short of it. If the switch replicates, bc null + strip
      flip reproduces the whole shape in three draws.
