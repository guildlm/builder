OCCURRENCE — builds whose captured output actually contains a timestamped line
    9 / 654   1.4%

WHY THE TWO DIFFER, and it is not a contradiction
    Emitting requires the tests to RUN and the logging path to execute. Most builds either converge
    before a failing test runs, or fail at compile and never reach execution. So the defect is latent
    in a sixth of the corpus and fires in about one build in seventy.

    ⚠️ THE CONCENTRATION IS THE POINT, NOT THE AVERAGE. ledger is 22 of 22 — every single tree — and
    ledger is the spec this campaign draws most and the one both parity arms used. taskapipro and
    workapi are zero of a hundred and four; nothing in those two specs can ever trigger it. An
    average of 17% describes no spec in the corpus.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE THREE CONSUMERS
    For workapi and taskapipro, `_error_signature`, `_error_keys` and `_canonical_toolchain_output`
    are all correct today and would gain nothing from the queued fix.
    For ledger, any build that fails AFTER its tests run hits all three at once — which is exactly
    what arm-1 did (8 surfaces, all distinct, three extension rounds, and a repair refused on log
    output).

    So the queued normalisation is not a 1%-of-builds fix. It is a 100%-of-ledger-failures fix, and
    ledger is where this campaign spends its draws.
