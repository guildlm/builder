FINDING — a failing build's repair and warning print AFTER its terminator, and my segmenter cut them off
=========================================================================================================
31 July, one hour after shipping _logseg.py. Logs only, no GPU. Caught by a contradiction, which is
the second time today that has been the detector rather than suspicion.

THE CONTRADICTION
    ledger-parity-arm-1 is a FAILING build and its log plainly contains
    "WARNING: internal/store/memory.go came out EMPTY (build never went green)".

    A sweep over the archive, filtering segments on "has an outcome marker", reported:

        failing builds mentioning an empty planned file        0

    A build I had read that afternoon was not in its own count.

WHY — THE TWO OUTCOMES DIFFER IN SHAPE, and I had only checked one
    GREEN      [guildlm-build]   WARNING: x shipped EMPTY — a sibling did its job
               Generated taskapi into ./generated/taskapi-v4        <- terminator LAST
               === INDEPENDENT verify in ... ===                    <- not this build's tail

    FAILING    [guildlm-build] exhausted 8 fix rounds (5 budgeted), still failing   <- terminator
               [guildlm-build]   left internal/store/memory.go empty: moving MemStore ...
               [guildlm-build]   WARNING: internal/store/memory.go came out EMPTY ...

    On a green build the warning precedes the terminator and is safely inside the segment. On a
    FAILING build the repair pass and FIX 1's warning run AFTER it — so a failing build's most
    interesting lines fell into the next segment, which has no outcome marker, and every consumer
    that filters on "is this a completed build" discarded them.

    ⚠️ THE BUG WAS IN THE FIX FOR THE PREVIOUS BUG. _logseg.py exists because start-based
    segmentation missed 160 builds. Terminator-based segmentation sees all 853 and then truncates
    each failing one. Both segmenters were checked against green builds and neither against the
    failing path — the path FIX 1 was built to open this morning.

TWO MORE DEFECTS ON THE WAY TO FIXING IT
    1. `BUILD_END` compiled `^Generated \\S+ into ` WITHOUT re.M. That anchors to the start of the
       whole STRING, so it matched fine scanning line by line and FAILED scanning a multi-line
       segment — every green segment read as unterminated, and the merge collapsed entire logs into
       one build. The self-test caught it on the first run.

    2. My first repair merged whole segments, which glued a failing build's tail onto the FRONT of
       the NEXT build. The self-test's "tail does not leak into the next build" case caught that
       too. Both cases existed only because I wrote them before believing the code.

THE RULE THAT WORKS, taken from the line shapes rather than guessed
    After a terminator, keep attaching INDENTED builder lines (`[guildlm-build]` + 2 spaces); stop
    at the first line that is not one. A new build's output and the `=== INDEPENDENT verify` blocks
    are never indented builder lines; the tail always is.

VERIFIED — segment count agrees with the independent outcome count on every real log tested
        ledger-parity-arm-1      segments  1 · with outcome  1 · build_count  1   AGREE
        ledger-stampfix-arm-4    segments  1 · with outcome  1 · build_count  1   AGREE
        FULL-SUITE               segments 23 · with outcome 22 · build_count 22   AGREE
        iso-shortener-07170020   segments  5 · with outcome  4 · build_count  4   AGREE
        sweep-v5-07290011        segments 24 · with outcome 23 · build_count 23   AGREE

    (The extra segment in each multi-build log is the trailing verify/summary block, correctly
    holding no outcome marker.)

RE-MEASURED — the EMPTY-planned-file defect per build
        completed builds                              853    (green 596 · failing 257)
        mentions an EMPTY planned file                 22    (2.6%)
            in GREEN builds                            19    (3.2% of green)
            in FAILING builds                           3    (was 0)

    AND THE THREE ARE EXACTLY THE POST-FIX-1 BUILDS: ledger-parity-arm-1, arm-2 and arm-3, all drawn
    today after the warning was moved out of _finish_green(). The other 254 failing builds were
    structurally unable to report, which is the claim FIX 1 rests on — now confirmed by which builds
    do report rather than only by which do not.

⚠️ "1.2% OF FAILING BUILDS" IS NOT A RATE AND MUST NOT BE QUOTED AS ONE
    The denominator 257 spans the whole archive; the numerator can only come from the four failing
    builds drawn since FIX 1. Among those four, THREE had an empty planned file and the fourth
    (arm-4) did not — because its repair succeeded. Same spec, same switch, one server process: that
    is n=1 as a condition, not a sample of failing builds.

    What it does suggest, and only suggest, is that the blind spot FIX 1 opened is NOT small. The
    honest statement is that the failing-build rate is UNMEASURED, and that the first four
    observations of it are not reassuring.
