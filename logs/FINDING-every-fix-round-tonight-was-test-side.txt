AND TWO OF THE THREE CAUSES WERE MINE

  taskapipro — I added TestValidateRejectsZeroDefaultPageSize to the test entry yesterday
  and never added the matching guard to the implementation entry. Three earlier draws
  inferred it; this one read literally and burned its whole budget. That is a spec I made
  unsatisfiable, and _promise_gap.py now finds the shape.

  taskapi — my empty-list closure introduced `strings` and a raw-body assertion into
  router_test.go, and the neighbouring healthz test copied the idiom into a place where it
  is wrong. Draw 1 of the same spec, without that import, decoded with json.Unmarshal and
  passed. Recorded in FINDING-the-idiom-spread-not-the-prose.txt.

  shortener — not mine. The spec says "GET /r/+created.Code EXACTLY ONCE (this is what
  increments Hits)": a setup step, not an assertion point. The model asserted on it anyway
  and expected the wrong code, since 301 is exactly what the spec promises the redirect
  returns.

THE COMMON SHAPE, and it is the practical output of this file

All three are ASSERTIONS THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST — one demanded by a spec entry that no
implementation entry promised, one copied from a neighbour, one invented on a setup step.
None of the three was an assertion the spec asked for and the model got wrong.

    A spec's job is not only to name the assertions it wants. It has to leave no room for
    the ones it does not.

That is why taskapi's healthz entry now says "assert the status code and NOTHING ELSE, do
not touch the body" instead of the previous wording, which warned about the exact trap and
then permitted the check. Explaining a trap while allowing the action is not a constraint.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY

Four draws is four draws, and three of them are HTTP specs of similar shape. usersapi cost
nothing and it is the smallest. This is not evidence that implementations are reliable in
general — it is evidence about where the cost went on ONE night, in a session whose edits
were all test-side to begin with, which is a selection effect I should not pretend away:
I spent the day editing test entries, so test entries are where the damage appeared.

The number worth watching next is whether the two repaired specs (taskapipro's promise,
taskapi's healthz) come back cheap on their next draw. taskapipro draw 4 is already queued.
