WHAT IT SETTLES — THE THIRD QUESTION, AND FIX 1'S SHAPE
The repair only ever runs at `_finish_green`, so the operative population is the green one: WHEN THE
PASS GETS TO RUN, IT WORKS, 11 OF 11, WITH NOTHING REVERTED. The failure mode was never that the
move breaks the build.

gate — the arm deliberately not taken — would attempt 59 moves on red trees and revert all 59. The
minimal version (warning only, repair still green-gated) was not merely the cautious option; it is
the one the numbers support, and the number did not exist when the choice was made.
