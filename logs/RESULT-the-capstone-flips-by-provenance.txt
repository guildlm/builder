RESULT — how many of the capstone's flips are attributable to the REDRAW at all
================================================================================

16:54 on 29 July. Two claims were retracted in the previous hour for the same reason: a
quantity read off a tree that the fix loop had rewritten, compared against one it had not.
That failure is not specific to those two claims. The capstone compares 140 live-in-both rows
across v4 and v5 trees, and NOTHING in that comparison knew how much repair either side had
absorbed. This measures it for every artifact at once.

    A sweep's verdict ABOUT AN ARTIFACT is unaffected — the shipped tree is the deliverable
    and how it got there does not matter. What is affected is reading a v4-vs-v5 DIFFERENCE
    as a fact about the SPEC, which is exactly what the campaign's headline does.

WHAT SPOILS PROVENANCE, and what does not
    `fixing <file>`                   a MODEL rewrite — assertions, seeding, names. SPOILS.
    `deterministic fix in <file>`     goimports-class. Does not touch assertions. Does not spoil.
    `rebuilt a request ... (drained)`  replaces a request; counted separately because it
                                      changes how many items land without moving an assertion.

CORPUS-WIDE (_provenance_census.py, 23 artifacts with both generations)

    8  CLEAN BOTH      no model rewrite either side — differences are the draw's
    8  MISMATCHED      one side model-rewritten, the other not. THE RETRACTED SHAPE.
    7  PARTIAL         v5 clean, v4 log unmatchable

THE ONE THAT CANNOT, AND IT IS THE WORST-LOOKING CASE
usersapi's v5 draw took exactly one model rewrite, and it was of a TEST FILE:

    compile/test FAILED, fix round 1/5
      fixing users_test.go
    converged to green after fix round 1

That draw's only capstone flip is a DEFENCE GAINED — middleware.go `boundary >= -> >`, SURVIVED
in v4 and CAUGHT in v5. A model rewriting a test file and a previously-undefended site becoming
defended in the same draw is precisely the confound, and the two cannot be separated from
outside. The row is not evidence that a redraw gained a defence; it is equally evidence that
the fix loop wrote a better test.

⚠️ 11 OF THE v4 BASELINE TREES WERE RESTORED WHOLESALE
Every .go file in them carries one identical mtime — demo-small, jsonapi, logstats, numkit,
ratelimit, taskflow, tasks-api, usersapi, workapi, workerpool, plantedred. A draw writes files
seconds apart; every genuinely-drawn tree here has a spread of 43-879s. So those trees were
copied into place, their timestamps say when they were copied, and their build logs cannot be
identified by time at all.

    That is a standing corpus fact, not a bug in the census: the v4 half of the baseline is
    partly unauditable, and it will stay that way unless a draw records its own log path into
    the tree. Doing that is one line at generation time and would make every future census
    exact. NOT DONE TODAY — src/ is holding still while a control draw runs.

⚠️ WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. It does not retract the capstone. Six-up-and-two-down remains the
best available estimate of redraw drift and the argument for it is unchanged. It says the
estimate rests on eight rows of which two are fully controlled, and that a future campaign
wanting a defensible drift figure must match provenance BEFORE comparing, not discover it
afterwards. The instrument now exists: _provenance_census.py, and _twin_witness --log=.

METHOD NOTES, because two matcher bugs were found and fixed while building this
  * Keying on the newest FILE dated taskapipro-v4 and ledger-v4 a week after their draws —
    their go.mod alone was rewritten 07-26 15:19 by a later corpus fix. Keying on the newest
    .go file matches both exactly. That bug silently lost the campaign's namesake artifact.
  * A zero mtime spread means COPIED, not drawn, and the tool now says RESTORED rather than
    picking whichever log sits nearest a copy operation. Before that it reported 12 artifacts
    UNKNOWN, which was the symptom that led here.
  * One-sided evidence is still evidence: if the v5 tree was model-rewritten, the difference
    is unattributable whatever the v4 log says. Reporting those as UNKNOWN threw away four
    verdicts the evidence supported.
