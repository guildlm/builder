FINDING — the 58% stub rate is a MIXTURE over conditions, not a per-draw coin flip
=================================================================================

It corrects my own framing from 17:05 and 16:50.

WHAT I HAD BEEN SAYING
17:05: "a coin flip the campaign has been drawing on all month." 16:50: "the defect is a REROLL, not
a property of a server or a day." Both invited the reading that each draw independently stubs with
p≈0.58.

    _cachefix-workapi       n= 3   full full full

    ⚠️ ONE GROUP EXCLUDED: "workapi-v" (v4, v5) came out mixed, but v4 and v5 are different SPEC

    SIX OF SEVEN GROUPS ARE UNIFORM. Ten consecutive stubs. Twenty-six consecutive stubs. Under an
    independent p=0.58 per draw, 26 uniform outcomes has probability ~2^-26 for a fair coin and is
    still vanishing at 0.58. That is not a coin flip; within a condition the outcome is mostly
    DETERMINED.

THE CORRECTED CLAIM
The stub outcome is largely a property of the CONDITION (spec version + process + whatever else a
run fixes), not of the individual draw. Some conditions stub every time; some never do. The 58%
corpus figure is an AVERAGE OVER CONDITIONS, weighted by how many trees each condition happens to
have left on disk — it is not the probability that any given draw stubs.

    ⚠️ AND THAT MEANS THE 58% IS NOT A RATE ANYONE SHOULD PLAN AGAINST. It answers "what fraction of
    the artifacts on this disk are stubbed", which is a fact about the corpus. It does not answer
    "how likely is my next draw to stub", which is what "coin flip" implies and what I implied.

WHAT SURVIVES, AND IT IS THE PART THAT MATTERED
    1. THE STUB IS COMMON, not three unlucky trees. Six of seven workapi conditions stub uniformly
       or not at all, and most of them stub. That is worse than a coin flip for anyone relying on
       memory.go, not better.
    2. IT IS NOT A SERVER OR A DAY PROPERTY. _iso-workapi-without gives 2 full and 1 STUB across
       three replicates of ONE condition — so something below the condition level still varies, and
       today's measurement says what: replicates run as separate PROCESSES, and processes write
       different programs from identical input. That is the mixing mechanism, already measured.
    3. ARM 4 STANDS. An inert edit flipped ledger from non-stub to stub on ONE process — i.e. an
       edit can move a condition across the boundary. Condition-determinism and edit-sensitivity are
       compatible: the condition determines the outcome, and an edit changes the condition.
