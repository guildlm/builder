FINDING — _required_decls extracts nothing from the purpose that names five sentinels
=======================================================================================
31 July, found by following the cross-package sentinel defect back one more step. Specs and archived
snapshots only, no GPU. builder.py FROZEN under the parity run; measured, not applied.

WHERE IT CAME FROM — the sentinel defect has a layer underneath it
    The previous finding concluded that response.go and models.go "guess independently because the
    channel that would tell them was empty". THAT IS NOT THE WHOLE STORY. ledger's spec NAMES the
    sentinels, in models.go's own purpose:

        "Sentinel errors, all matchable with errors.Is:
         ErrInvalid, ErrNotFound, ErrExists, ErrUnbalanced, ErrInsufficientFunds."

    So models.go was TOLD the exact name and wrote `ErrInsufficient` anyway — a violation of its own
    purpose. response.go, which receives every file's purpose, used the long name and was RIGHT.

    ⚠️ THIS CORRECTS THE PREVIOUS FINDING'S FRAMING. "Two files guessing" is wrong: one file was
    told and complied, the other was told and did not. The API-block fix is still worth having —
    it would let a consumer see what was ACTUALLY declared — but the primary defect is upstream.

AND THE GATE THAT EXISTS TO CATCH EXACTLY THIS EXTRACTS NOTHING
        _required_decls(models.go purpose)  ->  set()

    Empty. The gate rejects a candidate "missing promised X" for every X it extracts, and from a
    purpose listing five sentinel errors and three struct types it extracts none.

WHY — the three extractors, read rather than assumed
        _REQUIRED_TYPE_RE      ([A-Z]\w*)\s+(?i:struct|interface)     "Foo struct" only
        _REQUIRED_BACKTICK_RE  `([A-Z]\w*)`                           a whole backtick = one name
        _REQUIRED_VARCONST_RE  \b(?:var|const)\s+([A-Z]\w*)           "var Foo" only

    ledger's purpose writes `Account{ID string, Name string}` — backticked, but the backtick holds a
    struct literal, so `([A-Z]\w*)` cannot match. And the sentinels are a bare comma-separated prose
    list with no keyword and no backticks. Every form this spec actually uses is invisible.

HOW BIG, measured against as-drawn evidence rather than a regex guess
    For each (tree, file) with a spec purpose: identifiers the file ACTUALLY declares, that the
    purpose names VERBATIM, that _required_decls did not extract.

        (tree, file) pairs with a purpose                              472
        ...missing at least one such declaration                       397   (84%)
        ...restricted to EXPORTED declarations only                    318   (67%)

        most-missed exported:  NewRouter 46 · ErrNotFound 45 · Chain 42 · Middleware 42 ·
                               ErrExists 39 · NewAccountHandler 24 · ErrInvalid 24

    ⚠️ 84% IS THE LOOSE NUMBER AND 67% IS THE ONE TO QUOTE. The first includes unexported names
    (statusRecorder, writeError, writeJSON) and `main`, which a gate about cross-file contracts has
    no obvious business demanding. Restricting to exported names is the conservative read and it is
    still two thirds.

    ⚠️ AND "NAMED VERBATIM" IS NOT THE SAME AS "SHOULD BE REQUIRED". A purpose may name a symbol a
    SIBLING owns — that is precisely why the gate subtracts sibling_decls at the call site. This
    measures what the extractor CANNOT SEE, not what it ought to demand.

WHY THIS IS NOT A ONE-LINE WIDENING, and why I am not proposing one
    logs/FINDING-the-generation-gate-demands-the-over-reach.txt already records the opposite failure:
    the gate demanding names the file should NOT declare, because "nothing in the extracted names
    says which entry OWNS Store and which merely names it". Widening the extractor makes that
    problem strictly worse in proportion.

    So the two findings bound the design from both sides:
        too narrow   the gate is blind to two thirds of the exported names the purposes promise
        too wide     it demands names a sibling owns, and rejects correct candidates

    A useful widening therefore needs OWNERSHIP, not just extraction — and FIX 2 today already
    carries the machinery for that (_disclaimed_decls, _foreign_owned_decls). That is a design
    question, not a regex, and it is recorded here rather than attempted at 20:40 with a six-draw
    experiment holding the file.

⚠️ WHAT I CHECKED BEFORE CLAIMING THIS, having failed to three times today
    `grep -rln _required_decls logs/*.txt` — eight files. The closest, "the generation gate demands
    the over-reach", describes the gate's over-reach and its subtraction rule; none of the eight
    records that the extractor returns EMPTY for the purposes this campaign's own specs are written
    in. The boundary is narrow and it is stated rather than assumed.
