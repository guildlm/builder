  FUNCTION redeclaration, which no tester-shadow gate would touch. The generic phrase
  covers two unrelated defects, and counting by phrase rather than by shape would have made
  today's gate look like an already-measured dead end.

  That is the same lesson as the sort/status shapes earlier today: a mutation SHAPE and a
  PROMISE are different things, and so are an error PHRASE and a defect.
