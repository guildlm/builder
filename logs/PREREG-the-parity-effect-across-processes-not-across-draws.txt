PREREG — repeat the parity pair across SERVER PROCESSES, which is the unit that actually varies
================================================================================================
31 July, written while the mis-designed version is still finishing. Supersedes the replication design
in logs/PREREG-withhold-the-parity-clause-from-an-interface-only-file.txt; the ENDPOINT and REJECT
conditions there stand unchanged.

WHY THE FIRST DESIGN WAS WRONG, measured rather than suspected
    Both parity controls produced BYTE-IDENTICAL projects — 19 files, two processes, 100 minutes
    apart. The whole pipeline is a deterministic function of (spec, flags, server process).

    So "3 pairs" was three copies of one observation. Meanwhile the campaign has measured that the
    SAME prompt in a DIFFERENT server process yields different files — today, by rendering the
    pre-today and post-today prompts for store.go and finding them byte-identical while the archived
    and fresh outputs differ. Process is the variance; draw is not.

THE CORRECTED DESIGN
    For i in 1..3:
        restart mlx_lm.server (fresh process, same model)
        draw CONTROL  (GUILDLM_ENABLE_RULES='')                     on specs/ledger-origorder.yaml
        draw ARM      (GUILDLM_ENABLE_RULES='interface_only_scope') SAME process, same flags
        record the pid with both rows

    Same six draws, same cost. The pair stays inside one process — that is what makes it a controlled
    comparison — and the REPLICATION crosses processes, which is what makes three of them worth more
    than one.

    ⚠️ The pair must not straddle a restart. That is the one thing this design can get wrong, and it
    is the same requirement the campaign already enforces with `_server_pid.sh` for reused controls.

SCORING
    PRIMARY   the control/arm difference (control SPLIT, arm CONSOLIDATED) reproduces in all three
              processes. Predicted 2 of 3, confidence 50%.

    ⚠️ 50% IS NOT HEDGING, IT IS THE HONEST NUMBER. I have exactly one process's worth of evidence
    and a documented history of process-level variation changing outcomes for identical inputs. A
    higher number would be borrowed from the byte-identical replication, which measures the pipeline
    rather than the effect.

    REJECT    if control and arm AGREE in any process — both SPLIT or both CONSOLIDATED — the effect
              is process-dependent and the single-process result must be reported as such, not as
              "the parity clause prevents consolidation".

    NOT AN ENDPOINT   convergence (rc). arm-1 failed to converge and both controls went green, but
              four files differ between the conditions and the failure could originate in any of
              them. Recorded, never interpreted.

WHAT IT STILL CANNOT REACH
    One spec. ledger-origorder only. The cross-spec measurement today put workapi at a far higher
    consolidation rate and showed that ledger's clean bimodality does not generalise, so nothing here
    licenses a claim about specs other than this one.

    ⚠️ AND IT CANNOT RUN YET. `src/builder.py` and `_parity_ab.sh` are frozen until the current pairs
    finish — every draw is a fresh process that re-reads the harness, and bash reads a running script
    incrementally. The queue after the run is: HEAD guard, app-log normalisation, stripper logging,
    then this.
