WHAT IS ACTUALLY NEW — WHY THE TWO ARE INERT, MEASURED RATHER THAN BUCKETED
"Neither" named a leftover. Rendering the cross-package block BEFORE and AFTER the change, for
every wrong-qualifier tree, says what the model was actually looking at:

    taskapipro-pairA-t1   service.ErrExists/ErrNotFound  owned by `store`  visible BEFORE: NO
    taskapipro-pairB-t1   same                                             visible BEFORE: NO
    taskapipro-preedit    same                                             visible BEFORE: NO
    workapi-pairA-w1      same                                             visible BEFORE: YES
    workapi-pairB-w1      same                                             visible BEFORE: YES

In the three taskapipro trees `store` declares its sentinels in a GROUPED block, so the old
extractor dropped them and the consumer saw no sentinel anywhere in the project. Guessing
`service` — the layer it calls — is what a model does with no information. The fix makes
`store`'s ErrExists and ErrNotFound visible, so those three are mechanically reachable.

In the two workapi trees `store` declares them SINGLE-LINE. They were in the consumer's prompt
all along, correctly attributed to `store`, and the model wrote `service.ErrNotFound` anyway.

📌 THE FIX CANNOT REACH THOSE TWO, AND THIS IS THE SAME SHAPE AS 31 JULY'S SHARPEST RESULT:
"the model sees the name three times, verbatim, and abbreviates it anyway". Here it sees the
right package and writes the wrong one. Two independent defects — one at the declarer, one at
the consumer — now each have a case where the information was present and lost anyway.

⚠️ SO THE CEILING ON THE SHIPPED CHANGE IS NAMEABLE BEFORE ITS DRAW LANDS:
        18 declarer-gap    the fix changes what the consumer SEES; whether the DECLARER then
                           writes the long name is untouched by it, and five targeted
                           regenerations on 31 July produced byte-identical output
         3 qualifier       mechanically reachable — information newly present
         2 qualifier       PROVABLY INERT — information was already present
An upper bound of 3 of 23 trees is what the mechanism alone supports. Anything beyond that has
to come from the consumer matching a declarer it can now see, which is the pre-registered
endpoint and is not settled by any of this.

⚠️ AND 23 CASES IS 23 CASES — a small, recent, ledger-heavy population, with the qualifier class
confined to two specs. That caveat is inherited verbatim from 31 July and has not improved.

📌 METHOD NOTE, because it nearly cost a paragraph. My first before/after table reported models'
`ErrInvalid` as invisible in BOTH conditions, which would have been a hole in the shipped fix.
It was my filter: `line.startswith("Err")` misses the single-line form, which the extractor emits
as `var ErrInvalid`. The fix was fine; the probe was wrong. Sixth instance of the campaign's most
common failure, caught this time by a value that disagreed with a file I could open.
