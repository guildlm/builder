NOTE — five claims today: mechanism right, scope too wide. The corrections all came from counting.
===================================================================================================
31 July. A methodological note, written because the same shape recurred five times in one session and
the recurrence is more useful than any individual correction.

THE FIVE
    1. "workapi2 is the one GENUINE refusal in the run"
       Retracted: its store.go already held a bare `import "models"`; the move relocated an existing
       error. Zero genuine refusals in the corpus. What I had: a refusal message naming an import.
       What I did not do: open the donor file. One command.

    2. "the model DID write MemStore and its methods into memory.go; the stripper deleted them"
       Weakened: a 14-byte result proves only that every declaration in the candidate was one the
       sibling already had. A duplicate interface strips to 14 bytes too. Nothing in the record
       separates the two.

    3. "so the corpus numbers are a FLOOR again"
       Corrected 4 minutes later: 9 of 654 builds carry app-log lines, and v3 refused nothing at all,
       so no number was reduced. 57 of 57 stands as measured.

    4. "the failure mode is a switch between two whole shapes"
       Narrowed: true on ledger (1 symbol vs 9–11), false on workapi, whose clean files run to 9
       declarations and consolidated ones start at 7. The phenomenon generalised; my METRIC did not.

    5. "the whole pipeline is a deterministic function of (spec, flags, server process)"
       Corrected: the two arms diverge at the first fix round. The controls converged via
       deterministic gates alone, so they were structurally incapable of testing the part that varies.

THE COMMON SIGNATURE
    Every one stated a real mechanism and attached a scope the evidence could not carry. None of the
    mechanisms was wrong. In four of five, the evidence I had was drawn from cases that CANNOT
    exhibit the counterexample — green-only trees, ledger-only trees, controls that stopped before the
    model-driven loop.

    And the correction was the same move each time: COUNT THE POPULATION THE CLAIM RANGES OVER. Not
    re-examine the mechanism, not think harder — run one query over the archive or open one file.
    Each correction cost under two minutes; each claim would have been quoted.

WHAT TO DO WITH THIS, concretely
    Before writing a sentence with "so", "again", "always", "the whole", or "the failure mode is":
    name the population it ranges over and count it. If the evidence comes from one spec, say the
    spec. If it comes from cases that converged, say so — the cases that did not converge are exactly
    the ones that could refute it.

    ⚠️ This is not a resolution to be more careful. Four of today's five corrections were made BY me,
    within minutes, and the fifth within an hour — the discipline is already running. What is missing
    is doing the count BEFORE the sentence rather than after, and the count is the cheap part.
