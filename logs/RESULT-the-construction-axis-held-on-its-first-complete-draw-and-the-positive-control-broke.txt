7. INSTRUMENT CATCH — the ladder regex called G1 a rung on its first real row
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`_size_floor_tally.py` REFUSED the moment p7 was added: it declared G1 "other rewrite", the
derivation said "quantifier ladder", and it stopped rather than guess. The derivation was a
SHAPE-only test — `Sentinel error values, all <words> matchable` — and G1 ("all of them below")
was built to have exactly that shape minus the adverb. But the family it tests for is the ADVERB
ladder (15 August), whose five rungs are "readily/reliably/so readily/very readily/quite readily";
the regex just never encoded "adverb". It now requires the pad to END IN A -ly WORD. G1 and R1 are
NOT rungs (they are the AXIS's pad and replace), all five true rungs still derive as the ladder,
and every one of those facts is pinned in the self-test so the loosening cannot come back. A
non-adverbial pad having the ladder's shape is precisely what the axis exists to separate from it,
so the refusal was the tool doing its job on the first arm that could trip it.

Same shape as 15 August's catch (the substring `"Sentinel error values, all "` swallowed L3/L4)
and the fix is the same in kind: a signature that matches the WHOLE construction, not a fragment
of it. `_axis_hunt.sh` also had its ELIGIBLE line print the label index as a probe count ("after
25 baseline probes" for a first-probe find); label and classifying-probe count are now separate.
