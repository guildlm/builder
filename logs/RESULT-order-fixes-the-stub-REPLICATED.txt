STRENGTH — now stated for two pairs
If order were irrelevant, each B would be another draw from a distribution that stubs 40-67% of the
time, so each pair is ~2-3:1 and two independent pairs are roughly 4-9:1. That is meaningful and it
is still not proof. What carries more weight than the ratio is that the STRONG FALSIFIER has had
TWO chances and did not fire: the mechanism says B CANNOT stub, because memory.go is written before
store.go can take its job, and a single B-stubs observation kills it outright.

A DETAIL THAT WAS NOT PREDICTED AND IS WORTH RECORDING
    swapped-order memory.go:  2373 (p6) · 2374 (p7) · 2374 (arm 5, a third process)
    original-order store.go:  2588 (p6) · 2265 (p7)

Three different processes produce a swapped-order memory.go within ONE CHARACTER of each other,
while the original-order store.go that swallows the implementation varies by 300+. So the CORRECT
placement is highly reproducible across processes and the DEFECTIVE one is not — which is the
opposite of what "the whole tree rerolls per process" would suggest for an arbitrary file, and it
was not something I looked for.

    ⚠️ n=3 and noticed after the fact. Recorded as an observation to check on future pairs, not as
    a finding, and explicitly NOT folded into the order claim.

ALSO REPLICATED — the best-of-N gate rejects at the same point
Both B draws logged "rejected gen candidate 1: missing promised Store" at the same position in the
sequence. Two for two suggests it is a systematic consequence of generating memory.go before
store.go, not noise: the first candidate for one of the store files omits the promised symbol and
the gate catches it. The gate is doing its job in the swapped order; the point is that it fires
there at all.

WHAT IS NOW SUPPORTED
The memory.go stub — the MAJORITY outcome at 58% deduped across 107 trees, forbidden in words by
scope_rule which names it as its own example — is caused by ENTRY ORDER. The file generated first
takes the implementation, and the instruction does not stop it.

    THE FIX IS FREE AND MECHANICAL: emit implementation entries before the interface entries they
    satisfy. All three affected specs currently do the opposite (store.go 6th, memory.go 7th), which
    is exactly why the 107-tree corpus could never test it — the variable was constant.
