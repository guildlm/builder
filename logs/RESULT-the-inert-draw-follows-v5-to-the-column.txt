WHAT SURVIVES, precisely
The taskapipro comparison is unaffected: within THAT condition the control gave 0 of 8, and
that is the number the 2-of-8 across-condition result is measured against. What is withdrawn
is the generalisation. Byte-determinism is a property of the conditions measured — 8 draws
across three groups agreeing — and NOT of the pipeline. One group in four breaks it.

⚠️ AND THERE IS A CANDIDATE MECHANISM, WORTH NO MORE THAN THAT. The divergent arm is the one
with the completeness rule DISABLED; the arm with it enabled is 3-for-3 identical. A rule that
constrains what must be generated plausibly narrows the output distribution. N=3 per arm and
one divergent draw is far too little to claim it — but the arms ALTERNATE in time by design
(with, without, with, without), so at least this is not temporal drift, and that is more than
most of today's comparisons can say.

⚠️ IT ALSO PUTS A NUMBER ON WHAT A SINGLE REDRAW CAN MOVE. A draw that relocates a package's
implementation between two files changes every line number in both, which is precisely the
ordinal misalignment the capstone's site-count warning exists to catch. One draw in three, in
one condition.
