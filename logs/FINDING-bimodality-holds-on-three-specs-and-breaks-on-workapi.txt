FINDING — the all-or-nothing shape holds on three specs and BREAKS on workapi
=============================================================================
31 July. 140 trees, four spec families, parity trees excluded (one is live). Generalises — and
partly refutes — the bimodality noted on ledger an hour ago.

    spec          trees   store.go WITH MemStore        store.go WITHOUT
    ledger          22     8   (9–11 declarations)      14  (exactly 1)        clean split
    taskapi         19     7   (11–13)                  12  (1–6)              gap 6 -> 11
    taskapipro      44    12   (11–13)                  32  (0–3)              gap 3 -> 11
    workapi         55    42   (7–10)                   13  (1–9)              ⚠️ OVERLAP

    ledger is perfectly bimodal: a consolidated store.go declares nine to eleven symbols, a clean one
    declares exactly one. taskapi and taskapipro have a wide gap. workapi does NOT — its clean
    store.go files run up to NINE declarations while its consolidated ones start at SEVEN.

WHY workapi DIFFERS, and it is not mysterious
    workapi's store.go purpose legitimately assigns it more than an interface: "Exported sentinels
    var ErrNotFound and var ErrExists" plus a context-aware Store interface. So a correct workapi
    store.go declares several symbols, and declaration COUNT stops separating the two shapes there.

    The MemStore test still separates them cleanly on all four specs. It is the count-based
    "bimodality" that is spec-specific, not the underlying phenomenon.

    ⚠️ SO THE HOUR-OLD NOTE OVERSTATED ITS REACH. It said the failure mode "is a switch between two
    whole shapes" on the evidence of 11 ledger trees. On 140 trees the switch is still visible, but
    the metric I used to see it only works where the spec gives store.go a single job.

THE CONSOLIDATION RATE BY SPEC, since the counting was already done
    workapi 42/55 (76%) · taskapi 7/19 (37%) · ledger 8/22 (36%) · taskapipro 12/44 (27%)

    ⚠️ THIS IS A MIXTURE, NOT A PER-DRAW PROBABILITY. These trees span months, several harness
    versions, both entry orders, and deliberate experimental arms — including arms drawn SPECIFICALLY
    to produce or suppress the defect. Reading 76% as "workapi consolidates three times in four"
    would be exactly the error this campaign logged on 17:05 of 20 July: describing a mixture over
    conditions as a probability per draw.

    What it does support is the coarse ordering the campaign already had from stub rates (35/67/80 by
    spec): the defect is spec-dependent, and workapi is the worst affected.
