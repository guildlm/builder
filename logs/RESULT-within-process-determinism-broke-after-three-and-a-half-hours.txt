SO THE MODEL THAT FITS IS PROPAGATION FROM ONE ROOT EVENT, NOT REPEATED NONDETERMINISM
    File 5 diverged. Files 6-10 embed their predecessors in their prompts, so they inherit the
    difference. File 11 embeds it too and is insensitive to it, so it comes out identical.

    That is ONE nondeterministic event followed by deterministic propagation — a much smaller claim
    than "the process became nondeterministic", and it is what the recovery is evidence for.

    ⚠️ NOT PROVEN. Separating "one root event plus propagation" from "repeated drift that happened to
    miss file 11" needs a re-run with the predecessors pinned, which is not possible without
    instrumenting generation. Recorded as the model the evidence FITS, not as the mechanism.

⚠️ AND THE TWO TREES ARE MATERIALLY DIFFERENT PROGRAMS, not cosmetically

⚠️⚠️ THE PROPAGATION MODEL IS REFUTED — store.go's PROMPT IS IDENTICAL AND ITS OUTPUT IS NOT
