  Every durable closure today gave the invariant ITS OWN NAMED TEST. Every unstable one

  The repair is therefore not more emphatic prose — it is TestListReturns200 and
  TestGetReturns200 as their own named entries. That is a prediction, not a plan: if a
  dedicated named test also drifts, the ceiling is lower than this file claims and the whole
  naming lever is weaker than four closures made it look.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE SPEC-WRITING RULE
  An invariant that matters gets its own named test asserting ONE thing. Not a clause in
  another test's description, however precisely worded — "ASSERT rec.Code == http.StatusOK
  as its OWN step, BEFORE decoding" is as explicit as prose gets, and it was dropped on the
  next draw while the test around it survived intact.
