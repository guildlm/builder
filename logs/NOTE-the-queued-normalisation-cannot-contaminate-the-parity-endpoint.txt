CONCLUSION
    The parity endpoint is decided at GENERATION, before any toolchain output exists. Item (2) only
    touches text produced by running the toolchain, so it cannot move the endpoint of item (4).

    So the order is a COST decision: doing (2) first stops each failing ledger arm from burning three
    extension rounds it cannot escape, which is 100% of ledger failures per the capability
    measurement. That is a real saving on a six-draw experiment and no risk either way.

    ⚠️ What (2) WILL change in (4) is the convergence outcome — rc, round count, and which files the
    fix loop touches. Convergence is not an endpoint of the parity experiment and was excluded in the
    pre-registration precisely because four files differ between the conditions. That exclusion is
    what makes this independence hold; without it, (2) would be a confound.
