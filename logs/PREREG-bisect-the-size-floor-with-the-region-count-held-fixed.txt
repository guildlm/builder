    Two things in that table matter and only one of them has been said before:

    1. The gap +15..+19 has NEVER BEEN DRAWN. Not once, on any process, in the whole campaign.
       "On-line edits of +20 or more flip" is a statement about a range whose lower boundary was
       never probed — the boundary was inherited from which arms happened to exist.
    2. The tally is POOLED across processes whose wording->outcome maps demonstrably differ (the
       same +5 arm is ABBREVIATED on p1, LONG on p2, ABBREVIATED on p3). So it can motivate an
       experiment and cannot locate a floor. The floor must be bracketed against ONE process's
       OWN anchors, drawn in the same session.

    And the size account has a second confound that has never been removed: the big arms also
    touch MORE OF THE LINE. Every arm at +20 or above rewrites two or more regions; the small
    ones mostly touch one. Region count and size have moved together in every arm ever drawn
    except one pair — L6 (+14) and the paraphrase (+20), which are both TWO-region.

    region identity and the byte-identical name list are held; the adverb is not, and cannot be.

PROTOCOL (both gates unchanged, in this order, no exceptions)
    1. Probe fresh processes with specs/ledger-origorder-baseline.yaml until one returns ABSENT.
       Max 10 probes. LONG is uninformative. ABBREVIATED-at-baseline is informative under the
       standing definition but DISCARDED here for the same reason as 10 and 11 August: this
       ladder measures movement FROM an ABSENT baseline and cannot be read from halfway up.
       Every probe, kept and discarded, goes in logs/PROBE-LEDGER.txt.
    2. SCREEN the ABSENT process with specs/ledger-ownplacebo.yaml (+54 rendered, content-free,
       same purpose, LARGER than every arm below). A process that flips is DISCARDED and
       recorded. Only ABSENT-at-baseline AND screen-null may host arms.
    3. On the eligible process, RESTART=0 throughout, IN THIS ORDER:

           base (= the probe from step 1) · screen +54
           · L6 +14 (low anchor) · paraphrase +20 (high anchor)
           · F4 +20 · F2 +17 · F1 +15 · F3 +19
           · base-close

       THE ANCHORS RUN FIRST because without them on THIS process the rungs are uninterpretable —
       that is the whole lesson of 10 August, and a session that ends early must end with the
       anchors drawn rather than with four rungs and nothing to bracket them against. F4 is the
       first rung because it is the sharpest single arm. The bisection order after it is
       midpoint-first (+17), then the ends (+15, +19).

BRANCHES — written now so no result can be read into whatever I hoped for

           wordings both reach LONG. That is the strongest form the size/location account has ever
           had, and the practical rule sharpens to "rewrite the declaration line by about twenty
           characters", independent of what the rewrite says.
    F4 NOT LONG and paraphrase LONG
        -> SIZE IS NOT SUFFICIENT at fixed region count. Two arms of the same size in the same two
           regions land differently, so the words are doing work at a resolution below anything
           this campaign has resolved. This is the outcome that most damages the account I am
           working from, and it is the one I would report first.
    F4 LONG and paraphrase NOT LONG
        -> the high anchor failed to replicate on a fourth process; the ladder is then bracketed
           by F4 itself and the whole series is reported as anchor-conditional.
    a boundary inside the gap, monotone
        -> the first floor this campaign has ever located, and it gets ONE rung redrawn either
           side of the boundary before it is written down as a number.
    a boundary inside the gap, NON-monotone
        -> the fourth non-monotonicity. "Floor" becomes the wrong word for it and the result is
           reported as a fourth demonstration that no wording-level size rule generalises.
    all four ABSENT with both anchors reproducing
        -> the floor is between +19 and +20 on this process, which is a suspiciously sharp place
           for it to be, and the honest reading is that the paraphrase's +20 does something the
           adverb family does not — i.e. the same conclusion as the F4-null branch, reached the
           long way. Redraw the paraphrase before publishing.
    anchors do NOT reproduce (L6 not ABSENT, or paraphrase not LONG)
        -> report the rungs against THIS process's anchors only, and record the pooled tally as
           confirmed process-mixing. Do NOT relabel any arm against p1's or p3's anchors.
