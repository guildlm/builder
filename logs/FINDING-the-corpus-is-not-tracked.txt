FINDING — "the archive" is local state, not a durable baseline
===============================================================
2026-07-26. A correction to how I have been describing the corpus all session.

I have referred to generated/*-v4 as "the archive" and treated it as a fixed baseline —
the thing instruments measure against, the thing whose verdicts get quoted. It is
gitignored:

      .gitignore:2   generated/
      tracked files under generated/:  0

So it is LOCAL STATE. It survives only on this machine, it cannot be compared against
what it was last week, and anyone cloning this repo gets none of it. Every "the corpus
says" in today's notes means "this machine's copy says".

WHAT THAT CHANGES
  1. Promoting today's fresh trees into generated/ — which I just did for shortener,
     ratelimit, usersapi, jsonapi and taskflow, all verified green first — is not a
     recorded change. `git status` shows nothing. The old artifacts are simply gone.
  2. logs/hole-hunt-rows.tsv, tracked this morning after a retraction traced back to a
     lost row list, is therefore not merely convenient. It is the ONLY durable record of
     what the corpus said. That decision looks better now than the reasoning that produced
     it: I tracked it to make verdict drift visible, not knowing the corpus itself was
     unrecoverable.
  3. Several instruments dated artifacts by mtime. On untracked, regenerable state that is
     the only timestamp available, which is a sharper version of the caveat already
     recorded — there is no git history to fall back on for these files.

  Not proposing to track the corpus: it is ~25 Go projects of generated code, it is
  regenerable by definition, and the verdicts are what carry the meaning. But the
  distinction between "the baseline" and "my machine's current copy" needs to be visible
  in the notes, because I collapsed the two for a whole session.
