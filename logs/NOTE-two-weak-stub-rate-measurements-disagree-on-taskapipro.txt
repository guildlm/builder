WHERE THEY AGREE AND WHERE THEY DO NOT
    If 35/67/80 maps to ledger/…/workapi, then 35 vs 36 and 80 vs 76 line up closely. The middle
    value does not: 67 against taskapipro's 27 today.

    ⚠️ AND NEITHER NUMBER IS STRONG ENOUGH TO CALL THE OTHER WRONG.
      · the inherited one is n=3 by its own admission
      · today's is a MIXTURE over months, harness versions, both entry orders, and arms drawn
        specifically to produce or suppress the defect — I flagged it as such when I computed it
      · taskapipro's spec was edited 23 times, more than any other, so its trees span the most
        versions of the question

    The disagreement is therefore expected rather than informative. What it does do is put a number
    on how unstable this quantity is: two attempts at "the stub rate for taskapipro" differ by 40
    points, and both are defensible given what each measured.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, AND WHY IT IS NOT QUEUED
    Deliberate draws: one spec version, one process, N draws, count stubs. That is a clean category-1
    measurement and it would cost N × 20-80 minutes per spec.

    Not queued, because the campaign's actual question is not "what is the rate" but "what makes the
    model split correctly", and the rate has never been the thing under test. Recorded so that the
    35/67/80 in the save point is read as what it is — an open question with two weak, disagreeing
    estimates — rather than as an established distribution.
