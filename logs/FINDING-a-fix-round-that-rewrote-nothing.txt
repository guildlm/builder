    service_test.go, at the start of round 6      630904a7c1e2f467228cda8c19eb1808

The round ran `fixing internal/service/service_test.go`, drew its candidates, and wrote back

The repair is `fake := &fakeEnqueuer{}` and passing `fake`. Five consecutive rounds (2 3 4 5

not moving at all.

WHY THIS IS A BETTER STALL SIGNAL THAN THE ONE I SPENT THE MORNING ON
_stall_audit compares ERROR SURFACES between rounds and infers that no progress was made.
That is inference from symptoms, and it took three corrections today to get right —
consecutive-vs-set, a missing non-consecutive case, and successes counted as failures.

A hash of the fix target is not an inference. It is the thing itself:

    if the file the round set out to fix is unchanged after the round, the round
    accomplished nothing, and no further round has new information to work from.

Cheaper, exact, and immune to every framing problem that bit the surface comparison — a
package turning green elsewhere cannot disturb it, and neither can line numbers shifting or
errors reordering.

WHAT IS PROVEN AND WHAT IS INFERRED
  PROVEN   round 5's model fix wrote a byte-identical file. Two hashes, same value.
  INFERRED rounds 2-4 did the same. The evidence is the error's stability at 165:9 across
           all five rounds, which is strong but not a measurement — I did not start hashing
           until round 5. Recorded as inference.

THE REMEDY, alongside the four-word abort already queued
The abort-on-repeated-surface change deletes `rnd > max_fix_rounds and` so the existing
detector may fire inside the budget. This suggests a second, independent check that is
simpler than either: after a fix round, compare the target's hash. Unchanged means stop.

    Two signals, different in kind. The surface says "this failure has been seen before".
    The hash says "this round wrote nothing". The second cannot be confounded.

⚠️ BOUNDED, AND THE BOUND MATTERS. One artifact, one round, one hash pair. A no-op round is
not the same as a stall in general — a round can rewrite a file substantially and still fail,
which is what taskapipro did while oscillating between two self-inflicted defects. The hash
check would have said "progress" there every round while the run went nowhere. So it is a
sufficient condition for stopping, NOT a necessary one, and proposing it as a replacement for
the surface check would be wrong. It belongs beside it.
