WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE ARM DOES
The control is the arm that is supposed to REPRODUCE the defect. The pre-registration named this
outcome in advance: "if the fresh controls do not consolidate, the experiment has no defect to remove
and the arm result is uninterpretable — that is a real possible outcome and it is now written down as
one." One control is not three, but the contrast with 3-of-3 is not subtle.

WHAT I CANNOT YET SAY, and the candidates
    The spec text for this package is UNCHANGED since those draws (c7f7c10 touched one line in the
    models entry). What changed is the harness, three times, all in this package's generation path:
        FIX 2       memory.go's demand went from {Store} to {MemStore}
        _disclaimed_decls   removed Store from memory.go's demand
        the gate    stopped rejecting memory.go's candidate over Store

    ⚠️ EVERY ONE OF THOSE ACTS ON memory.go, WHICH IS GENERATED SECOND. None of them can change what
    store.go — generated FIRST — chooses to write. So the obvious causal story does not actually
    reach the file that used to over-reach, and I do not have a mechanism.

    The honest alternatives, in the order I would bet on them:
        1. sampling variation. Two candidates at temperature 0.1, and the archived 3/3 is three
           draws in ONE older process. Three fresh draws could differ by chance.
        2. something in today's changes reaches store.go by a path I have not traced —
           `_foreign_owned_decls` widened with FIX 2, which feeds store.go's redeclaration stripper.
        3. process-level variation: a different mlx_lm.server process, which this campaign has
           already documented as producing different generations for identical arms.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT, decided now rather than after seeing controls 2 and 3
    If all three fresh controls SPLIT: the arm is uninterpretable as pre-registered, and the result
    of this session becomes "the consolidation defect does not reproduce under today's harness on
    ledger-origorder" — which needs a bisect to attribute and must NOT be credited to the parity
    clause, which is not even removed in the control.

    If controls 2 and 3 CONSOLIDATE: control-1 was the outlier, the defect reproduces at 2/3, and the
    arm is interpretable against that rate.

    Either way the arm draws still run. They are already scheduled and their cost is sunk.
