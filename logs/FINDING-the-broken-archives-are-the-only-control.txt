FINDING — the two artifacts that look like corpus rot are the only negative control
====================================================================================
2026-07-26. Started as cleanup, ended as the opposite.

WHAT LOOKED LIKE ROT
  tasks-api-min-v4 and tasks-api-noshadownudge-v4 do not compile — handlers.go references
  ErrNotFound and no file defines it. They are archives of runs the builder gave up on,
  sitting in generated/ beside successful ones, and every instrument sweeps generated/*-v4
  indiscriminately. They accounted for 13 of the 150 probes in a headline that had been
  quoted all day as if all 150 were measurements.

  The obvious move was to delete or regenerate them.

WHAT THE CORPUS ACTUALLY SAYS
      all 13 BASELINE-RED rows            come from exactly these two artifacts
      what these two artifacts produce    13 BASELINE-RED, 32 NOAPPLY, and nothing else
                                          — never a CAUGHT, never a SURVIVED

  So they contribute zero evidence about holes, and they are the ONLY inputs that exercise
  the BASELINE-RED path at all. That path implements the rule TEETH.md calls central: a
  mutation whose UNMUTATED baseline is already red is not a CAUGHT, it is a void verdict.
  Delete these two and the rule still exists, still passes review, and is never executed by
  anything.

  A green suite would not notice. That is the same shape as everything else found today —
  a check that stops running looks exactly like a check that finds nothing — except here
  the trigger would have been TIDYING, which is the version nobody suspects.

ENFORCED, NOT JUST WRITTEN DOWN
  A test now asserts the corpus contains at least one artifact whose baseline is red, and
  says why in its failure message. Cleaning them up turns it red instead of quietly
  disarming the rule. The test does not care WHICH artifacts they are — regenerating these
  two is fine as long as something still exercises the path.

  Written as a test rather than a comment because a comment is exactly what a person
  tidying a directory of build artifacts does not read.
