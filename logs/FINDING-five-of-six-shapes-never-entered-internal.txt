FINDING — five of six mutation shapes never looked inside internal/
====================================================================
2026-07-28. Model-free. Frozen trees. Found while checking a route-coverage result.

HOW IT SURFACED

_route_coverage reported taskapipro's GET /healthz and GET /readyz as never requested. Both
are registered in internal/api/router.go and both write http.StatusOK. So I went looking for
the matching status rows in the sweep to see what the mutation said about them, and found
that taskapipro-v4 has NO rows for router.go at all. Not SURVIVED. Absent.

Then the general form:

    status/wrap ROWS in the 170-row sweep      sites those two shapes MATCH
      ledger-v4          0                       18
      taskapi-v4         0                       30
      taskapipro-v4      0                       38
      workapi-v4         0                       23

Every artifact with an internal/ layout contributed ZERO status rows and ZERO error-wrapping
rows to the sweep the whole campaign has been reading. The flat artifacts — taskflow,
tasks-api, usersapi, shortener, jsonapi — were swept fully, which is why nothing looked odd.

THE CAUSE

Six shapes, six copies of the same walk, and five of them said `glob` where the sixth said
`rglob`:

    wrap_rows     art.glob("*.go")      top level only
    sort_rows     art.glob("*.go")      top level only
    tag_rows      art.glob("*.go")      top level only
    header_rows   art.glob("*.go")      top level only
    status (main) art.glob("*.go")      top level only
    bound_rows    art.rglob("*.go")     the whole tree

`glob("*.go")` on an artifact laid out as internal/api/..., internal/store/... matches
nothing at all. Not a partial sample — nothing.

AND THE COVERAGE CHECK AGREED WITH IT

This is the part worth keeping. The sweep has a coverage counter whose entire purpose is to
report sites matched versus sites probed, so under-sampling shows up as a gap. It had a
`recurse` parameter, defaulting to False, and exactly one caller passed True — the boundary
shape. Its own comment records why:

    "`search` and `recurse` exist because the boundary pattern needs both and the counter
     reported `matched 0 probed 9` without them ... bound_rows walks nested packages with
     rglob while this walked only the top level."

I had noticed the discrepancy, for one shape, and resolved it by teaching the COUNTER to
match the shape. For the other five, counter and shape both walked the top level, matched
equalled probed, and every one reported as fully covered.

    A coverage number computed over the walk can only ever report the walk back to you.

That is the third instrument in this repo whose completeness check was calibrated to the
thing it was built to catch, and the most expensive: the capstone's "170/170, 100% of probes
answering" is true of the rows that exist and says nothing about the rows that were never
created. The denominator was the walk.

WHAT IT COSTS — MEASURED, ONE ARTIFACT

workapi-v4, swept with the repaired walk (--all-sites):

    recorded in the tracked sweep      6 rows
    with the walk fixed               36 rows, 36 of 36 answering, 16 SURVIVED

Sixteen candidates on one artifact, none of which had ever been asked. Extrapolating is not
a measurement, so I am not going to publish a corpus number until the full re-sweep runs —
but the four nested artifacts hold 109 status/wrap sites between them, plus tag, sort and
header sites, against a sweep that currently totals 170 rows for the whole corpus.

WHAT IT DOES NOT COST

Every CAUGHT verdict already graded stands. The closures were graded per site with explicit
file pins, and the boundary/clamp shape — the one that carried this week's work — was the
ONE shape that already recursed. Nothing measured is retracted. What changes is the claim
about COMPLETENESS: "the corpus has 19 real holes" was always "19 among the rows we made",
and five shapes were making none for four artifacts.

Sixteen SURVIVED is also not sixteen holes. Six of workapi's are
StatusInternalServerError->StatusBadGateway, which is the class the capstone adjudicated as
seventeen DEAD 500-else branches on unreachable lines. The adjudication work is ahead, not
behind. But it cannot start on rows nobody generated.

THE SECOND BUG, FOUND BY FIXING THE FIRST

Switching to rglob made every nested site report SKIP. The shapes passed `f.name` — the
BASENAME — as the file to mutate, which is identical to the relative path only when the file
sits at the top level. So the first fix produced "internal/api/middleware.go matched" and
"middleware.go SKIPPED", and the tool was right to refuse: it could not find the file it was
asked to mutate. Failing toward SKIP rather than toward a silent mis-mutation is the correct
direction, and it is the only reason this was a five-minute problem rather than a retraction.
All five shapes now address by artifact-relative path.

THE FIX

One `go_files(art)` used by all six shapes AND the counter, because six copies of a walk is
what allowed five of them to drift. The `recurse` flag is gone: the corpus is the corpus, and
if a shape under-samples the gap now appears in the coverage line, which is the entire reason
that line exists. Pinned in the self-test with a nested fixture, and the pin verified by
reverting to glob and watching it fail.
