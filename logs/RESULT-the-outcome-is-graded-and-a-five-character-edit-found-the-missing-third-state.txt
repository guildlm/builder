⭐ THE MISSING THIRD STATE, FOUND — AND NOT WHERE ANYONE WAS LOOKING FOR IT
    ABBREVIATED is a third of the ledger archive (9 of 25 trees). It has been a registered,
    UNRUN branch since 6 August, hunted by PROCESS PROBES: six on 6 August and about ten today,
    every one of them ABSENT or LONG, never once ABBREVIATED. The save point says in bold that
    ABSENT must not be substituted for it.

    It was never a property of a process to be found by probing. A FIVE-CHARACTER SPEC EDIT
    produced it on demand.

⚠️⚠️ AND IT MEANS MY OWN ANALYSIS WAS SCORING A THREE-VALUED OUTCOME AS TWO-VALUED
    Every arm in this ladder was reasoned about as FLIP or NULL — I compared sha256 against two
    known files and called anything else unthinkable. The rule I had just preregistered (a COUNT
    of edited regions: one null, two or more flip) was built on that binary, and L4 does not fit
    it: two regions, but the outcome is neither.

    What actually fits all eight arms is a DOSE-RESPONSE ON AN ORDERED OUTCOME:

        ABSENT  <  ABBREVIATED  <  LONG

    one region at any size tested -> ABSENT · two regions at +5 -> ABBREVIATED · two regions at
    +20 and above -> LONG. Both the count and the size move it, and they move it ALONG A SCALE
    rather than throwing a switch. "A switch, not a slope" was a description of a ladder whose
    rungs were all at the top.

    📌 The only reason this was visible at all is that the CLASSIFIER is three-valued, and it is
    three-valued because 31 July made "ask the three-way question" a standing rule after a binary
    endpoint could not express its own baseline. The tool preserved the distinction that my
    reasoning had already discarded — and _declared_vs_spec.py, self-tested for exactly the
    rename case, reported MISSING ErrInsufficientFunds / EXTRA ErrInsufficient rather than
    quietly scoring "5 declarations, fine".
