PREREG — stop demanding a symbol the entry itself disclaims
===========================================================
Written before the edit, 31 July morning. Follows
logs/FINDING-the-generation-gate-demands-the-over-reach.txt, which measured one ambiguous symbol per
spec (memory.go required to declare `Store`, which store.go owns) and 16 of 17 archive rejections
where the gate was never satisfied and the harness shipped the candidate it had just called dirty.

THE THING I DID NOT EXPECT TO FIND, AND WHICH CHANGES THE FIX
The FINDING said disambiguating ownership "needs a verb distinction the specs use but nothing
checks". Reading the eight purposes verbatim, the specs do not merely imply it — they SAY it:

    ledger/memory.go      "the in-memory implementation of the Store interface DECLARED IN STORE.GO
                           IN THIS SAME PACKAGE"
    workapi/memory.go     "reference Store, ErrNotFound, ErrExists directly, DO NOT REDECLARE"
    taskapipro/memory.go  "reference Store, ErrNotFound, ErrExists directly, DO NOT REDECLARE"
    taskapi/memory.go     "implementation of the Store interface FROM STORE.GO ... DO NOT REDECLARE"
    every store.go        "The Store INTERFACE only — no implementation in this file" / "A
                           CONTEXT-AWARE Store INTERFACE"

    The ownership is written in the spec in plain English, and the extractor reads past it. That is
    a better fix than a verb heuristic and a worse look for the extractor.

THE RULE
    A symbol stays in `required` unless the SAME purpose that names it also disclaims it. Disclaimer
    markers, case-insensitive, searched in the purpose text: "do not redeclare", "declared in",
    "from store.go"-style "from <file>.go", "implementation of", "implements", "satisfy/satisfies".
    Applied only to symbols another SAME-DIRECTORY entry also promises — an unambiguous symbol is
    never dropped, whatever words surround it.

SCORING — static, derived, no draws
    MUST MOVE      internal/store/memory.go loses `Store` in all four specs (ledger, workapi,
                   taskapipro, taskapi) = 4 entries changed.
    MUST NOT MOVE  every store.go KEEPS `Store`. If a rule drops it there too, it is the symmetric
                   rule the FINDING warned about — it disarms the owner as well as the borrower —
                   and it MUST BE REJECTED, not tuned.
    MUST NOT MOVE  no other entry in the WHOLE spec directory changes its demand set. Any other
                   change means the markers are matching prose they should not, and the last edit
                   taught me that a three-spec check is not a spec-directory check.

    Endpoint: exactly 4 entries change, all memory.go, all losing exactly {Store}. Not "at least 4".

WHAT THIS CANNOT REACH — stated first, because it is the part that matters
    The archive says the model REFUSED this demand in 5 of 6 occurrences and the harness shipped the
    refusal anyway. So the gate is not what puts the sibling's content in the wrong file; the model
    is, five times out of six. This edit buys one saved draw per occurrence and removes the 1-in-6
    path where the gate itself pushed the interface into the implementer.

    PREDICT THEREFORE: the stub/consolidation rate on artifacts is UNCHANGED, within noise. If a
    later draw shows it falling, that is a result about something else and must not be credited
    here. Confidence 85% — the 15% is the 1-in-6 path being commoner in shapes the archive
    under-samples.

    ⚠️ AND NOTHING HERE ADDRESSES THE OPEN QUESTION. How to make the model split a package's content
    correctly is still unanswered; `scope_rule` forbids consolidation in words and fails 58% of the
    time, entry order only chooses the victim, and this gate is a bystander that was making it
    marginally worse.
