FIX 1 moved the empty-planned-file warning out of `_finish_green` so it runs on every exit. This
prices that change against the archive: how many builds ended NOT green, and therefore said nothing?

    BUILDS, segmented at "generate <file> (1/N)"
        green                    454

    Zero is the expected number and the reason to trust the segmentation: before FIX 1 the warning
    was structurally unreachable on a failing build. Every one of those 160 was silent about whether
    the project it gave up on matched its own plan.

TWO CORRECTIONS I HAD TO MAKE TO MY OWN COUNT BEFORE IT MEANT ANYTHING
    1. I first counted LOGS, not builds: green 413 / failing 216. Driver and suite logs hold several
       builds each (19 such logs), so a single file can carry both outcomes and was being counted as
       whichever regex hit first. Segmenting per build moved the numbers to 454/160.
    2. Even segmented, ONE failing build appeared to carry a warning — impossible by construction, so
       I opened it instead of rounding it off. FULL-SUITE.log, build 16 of 16: the warning belongs to
       a green taskapi build, and the suite's trailing RESULT summary (including another spec's
       failure text) falls inside that last segment because nothing marks a build's END. The count is
       0, and the segmentation has a known edge: the final segment of a multi-build log runs to EOF.

    ⚠️ THE OUTLIER WAS THE ONLY THING WORTH EXAMINING IN THE WHOLE MEASUREMENT. Both counts would have
    been quoted without it, and both would have been wrong — the first materially (216 vs 160).

WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT PRICE
    ESTABLISHED: 160 of 650 terminal builds (26%) ended failing, and plan conformance was
    unreportable on all of them. That is the population FIX 1 newly covers.

    NOT ESTABLISHED: how many of those 160 actually shipped an empty planned file. That needs the
    trees, most of the 160 no longer have one, and the corpus is moving. The warning rate among green
    builds is 11 of 454 (2.4%), but green builds are exactly the ones where the repair ALSO ran and
    may have fixed the file before the warning could fire — so 2.4% is a floor for green builds and
    tells us nothing directly about failing ones.

    ⚠️ SO THIS DOES NOT SAY "160 SILENT DEFECTS". It says 160 builds where the check could not speak,
    of which an unknown subset had something to say. Todays' earlier over-claim — attributing all 11
    survival fills to FIX 2 when 6 were its doing — is the same shape, and it is the reason this
    paragraph exists.
