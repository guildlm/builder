  Not "a closure failed" — single failures are ordinary. The results that would change
  how I write specs:

  a. The Chain test is written in all five and CAUGHT in fewer than three. That would
     mean the naming rule works but the ASSERTION-DICTATION does not, and the seven
     earlier closures were easy assertions rather than well-named ones.
  b. bitset writes 128 and the mutation still SURVIVES. That would mean I have the
     boundary arithmetic wrong, and the whole "witness off the boundary" finding needs
     re-deriving rather than re-stating.

     drops cluster in the ones that received TWO edits (taskapipro, workapi, tasks-api)
     rather than one, that is the first evidence for edit-count as a cost, which six
     refuted hypotheses have failed to find.
