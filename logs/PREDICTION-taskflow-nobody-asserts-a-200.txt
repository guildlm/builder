THE RULE UNDER FOUR OF THE FIVE — DECODING IS NOT ASSERTING
  TestList, TestListSorted and TestProjectList all GET a collection and go straight to
  json.NewDecoder(...).Decode(&got). None of them reads rec.Code. A handler that answered
  202 Accepted with a byte-identical body would pass every one of them. The same is true of
  the single-GET on the tasks side: no test performs a successful GET of one task at all —
  TestGetMissing checks 404, TestDelete checks 404 after deleting.

  So four of the five holes are one habit: the test that exercises the happy path decodes
  the payload and never looks at the status line. That is a different failure from a missing
  test, and it is invisible to any count of test functions — ratelimit's holes were tests
  that did not exist, and these are tests that exist and do not look.
