NOTE — grading the shipped tree measures the DRAW, not the fix loop: 21 of 21 agree
===================================================================================
31 July. A condition the parity A/B's verdict depends on, checked while control-1 was still drawing.

THE WORRY
`_parity_ab.sh` grades the FINISHED tree: does store.go declare MemStore, does memory.go. But the
split is decided during GENERATION, and between generation and the finished tree sit the fix loop,
the redeclaration stripper's later passes, and — as of today — `_fill_empty_planned_files` on BOTH
exits, which exists precisely to MOVE declarations between those two files. If any of that can flip
the verdict, the grader is measuring the harness's cleanup rather than the model's choice.

THE CHECK
For every tree carrying a .pre-fix.json (the as-drawn snapshot) and a store package, grade both the
snapshot and the shipped tree, and compare. Parity trees excluded — one is being written right now.

    21 trees · as-drawn verdict == shipped verdict: 21 · CHANGED: 0

    Not one tree in the corpus has its store-package split altered between the draw and the ship.

WHAT IT LICENSES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
    LICENSES: the parity A/B's endpoint is a statement about what the MODEL wrote. Reading the
    finished tree is equivalent to reading the draw, on every case where both exist.

    DOES NOT: this is 21 trees, all drawn BEFORE the repair was wired into the failing exit today.
    A red tree that now gets its planned file filled on the way out is exactly the case that could
    break the equivalence in future — and none of the 21 was drawn under that code. The parity draws
    are, so if a later pair disagrees between snapshot and ship, this NOTE is where to look first.

    ⚠️ Stated because the check passing is not the same as the check being future-proof, and the
    change most likely to break it was made today by me.
