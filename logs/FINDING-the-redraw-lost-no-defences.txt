  Every -v4 tree regenerated from the same specs, same model, same settings, a week after

  comparison (_redraw_diff.py, keyed by artifact+file+shape+ordinal).

WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY
  NOT A CONTROLLED A/B. Several specs were EDITED between the two sweeps — that is what the
  three gains are: the %w closures written this week landing on a fresh draw. The comparison
  is "the corpus as it was" against "the corpus as it is", not the same spec twice.

  THE NEW CORPUS IS HEALTHIER. All twenty specs regenerated green, where the old corpus held
  broken archives, so more probes can answer (63 of 104 versus 64 of 150). A higher answer
  rate is not more defence.

  ELEVEN SURVIVORS REMAIN, and they are the work queue: ratelimit's Retry-After header and
  its 429, ratelimit api.go's two StatusOK sites, taskflow's projects_handler 400/409/200,

  in the OLD-spec tree and CAUGHT in the tree built from the corrected spec
