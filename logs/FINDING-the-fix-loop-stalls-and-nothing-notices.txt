FINDING — when the fix loop repeats a failure it never recovers, and it spends the budget anyway
================================================================================================

Measured 29 July ~12:00 across all 20 frozen v5 logs. Run because the previous finding's
generalisation check was written down as deferred — on the stated grounds that "the corpus
is still moving". That reason was wrong: only taskapipro is moving, the other twenty logs
have been frozen since 02:08, and the check was available the whole time. Running it changed
the previous finding's standing.

  bitset      2 rounds   PROGRESS  round 1 panic (index out of range) -> round 2 a
                                   DIFFERENT failure (u.Test(130) = false) -> GREEN.

  taskapi     5 rounds   STALL     rounds 2,3,4,5 all identical:

                                   after the model reverted the gate. Slow, but MOVING.

THE SIGNAL IS FREE AND NOBODY READS IT

    If round N and round N+1 report the SAME failure text, the loop has stalled and the
    remaining rounds will not recover it. Two for two here, and both shipped NOT-GREEN.

Wasted model rounds in this corpus: taskapi 3, expreval 4 — SEVEN rounds of best-of-N fix
calls that could not have helped, on a corpus of twenty specs. taskapi alone made 30 model
fix calls. The budget is spent to its last round every time because nothing compares this
round's failure to the last one.

An early abort on a repeated failure is a small change and it is the RIGHT small change:
it would not fix anything, but it would stop pretending to. A NOT-GREEN tree that stops at
round 2 is the same artifact as one that stops at round 5, minus four rounds of calls, and
it reports the stall honestly instead of burying it in a budget exhaustion.

THIS BOUNDS THE PREVIOUS FINDING, AND I SAY SO PLAINLY
FINDING-the-model-fix-reintroduces-what-the-gate-just-repaired.txt documents a
repair-regress cycle on taskapipro and asks whether it generalises. It does NOT. Neither
stalled spec shows it; both show a single unfixed failure repeating. The repair-regress
cycle is one observation on one spec, and against the corpus it is the LESS common pattern
and the more benign one — taskapipro is progressing, which taskapi and expreval never did.

    I framed taskapipro's slow rounds as the pathology. The corpus says the pathology is
    the specs that never move at all, and taskapipro is the healthy case.

WHAT THE STALLS WERE ACTUALLY ABOUT — and it is already recorded
taskapi's four repeated rounds are the nil-slice defect the empty-list closure caught. That
is the closure working exactly as designed: the test named the invariant, the invariant was
violated, and the suite refused to go green. The loop then could not repair it, which is
the separate known problem recorded at da248de ("the fix loop widens inside the failing
test's package, and the defect is one layer down"). So taskapi's NOT-GREEN is a SUCCESS of
the defence and a FAILURE of the repair, and those two facts have been reported as one
number all week.

expreval's is a genuine implementation defect — a parser returning 1 for "1+2" — with no
closure involved.

rate. And the abort criterion is a src/ change, so it waits — see
