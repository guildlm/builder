⭐⭐ 1. PORTABILITY IS NOT ABOUT WHAT THE EDIT SAYS
    Three rewrites of the declaration line that share NO content story all flip this process,
    and each writes the same bytes it wrote on p1:

        shipped    +51  "Exported sentinels" + `var ErrX` + "(each errors.New)"   -> ef8c15d6
        A+C        +21  title + parenthetical, names left BARE, no `var` at all   -> ef8c15d6
        paraphrase +20  adds NO information; name list byte-identical             -> 649bdef7

    A pure paraphrase and a `var`-showing rewrite cannot both be carrying the ingredient. The
    6 August rule ("show the declaration form") was demoted to a wrong DESCRIPTION on 10 August
    on p1 alone; this is that demotion, replicated on an independently screened process, with
    the shipped arm as its own positive control in the same series.

    What is portable is a FAMILY: substantially rewrite the declaration line, in any of the three
    tested ways, and the process declares the name. What that family shares is where it is,
    not what it says.
